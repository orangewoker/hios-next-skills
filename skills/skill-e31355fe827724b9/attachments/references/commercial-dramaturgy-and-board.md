# Commercial Dramaturgy and Shooting-Board System

> Status: source-backed active module promoted from S008 after five-class extraction, triple verification and pressure testing.
> Purpose: preserve product causality from brief through treatment, storyboard and model-facing prompts.

Use this module when developing product-led ideas, multi-feature structures, feature-bearing performance, claim carriers, treatment traceability or shooting-board readiness. It supplements—never replaces—the visual prompt, premium-look, causal-visual and complex-shot systems.

## 1. Product causal chain

Build the creative spine as:

`specific human/usage obstacle → intention and trigger → product intervention → observable response/proof → changed action, choice, relation or state → brand payoff`

- The obstacle must be visible and specific; a demographic “pain point” is not enough.
- Trigger and response must both be readable. An isolated UI tap or a result montage is not a chain.
- Proof precedes conclusion. VO, dialogue or Super may recover meaning only after the viewer has evidence.
- If removing the product leaves the same plot intact, the product is not the dramatic engine.
- Pure beauty, packshot and material films may use product-state choreography instead of inventing a human obstacle.

## 2. Feature-bearing behaviour and state hinges

Write behaviour from the character's immediate goal, not from the feature list:

`goal → body/prop/product start state → natural operation → product response → changed state → handoff`

A useful action can also become a state hinge: a door closes, a buckle locks, a surface is touched, a gaze selects or a device opens, and space, sound, attention or emotional state changes at the same physical event.

Track hands, gaze, weight, distance, product geometry, response latency and end pose. Do not let “actor taps screen” replace proof. For model handoff, encode preparation, contact/trigger, resistance/response, completion and landing.

## 3. Scenario friction modules

For films with several features, cluster capabilities by one continuous human task. Each module uses:

`friction → primary capability → trigger → response → proof window → character recovery → bridge`

- One module has one primary capability. Secondary capabilities remain only if caused by the same task.
- Modules share a character objective, prop, direction, location change or state progression.
- Do not use borrowed film identities, rapid costumes or unrelated world transformations as the organizing principle.
- Give each module a sequence state and explicit entry/exit handoff for generation batches.

## 4. Treatment-to-shot traceability

Create a two-way matrix:

| Treatment rule | Scope | Controlled shots | Prompt fields | Acceptance evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Forward audit: every approved proposition, performance, world, camera, light, material, edit, sound or AIGC rule needs downstream evidence.
- Reverse audit: every hero, proof or transition decision needs a brief source, approved rule or clearly labelled proposal.
- A rule with no shot is an orphan rule. A shot with no source is an orphan shot.
- Store global rules in the project bible, sequence rules in sequence state and local differences in shot deltas. Do not paste the treatment into every prompt.

## 5. Evidence grades and claim carriers

Register each capability, effect, ranking, health/safety statement, talent fact or research status in a Claim Ledger:

| Claim | Source/version | Evidence grade | Approval | Allowed carrier | Required disclosure | Shots/clocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Evidence grades are project-specific but must distinguish at least:

- released/implemented and verified;
- prototype or research-stage demonstration;
- dramatized reconstruction;
- inference or illustrative analogy;
- brand declaration.

Choose among observable picture proof, verified UI/data, VO/dialogue, authorized interview, Super and legal disclosure. A disclaimer cannot repair a fundamentally misleading hero image. Model prompts may include only approved visible behaviour and state; they must not visually upgrade a claim.

## 6. Dual-layer shooting-board audit

### Creative layer

- audience information/emotion before and after;
- primary story/persuasion/product job;
- start → change → result;
- character goal and product role;
- entry/exit and sequence contribution.

### Production layer

- duration and parallel clocks;
- subject/product start and end state;
- topology, viewpoint, frame and camera path;
- light/material/sound and continuity;
- references, dependencies, generation mode, risk and fallback.

Completeness in one layer cannot compensate for emptiness in the other. Record insurance coverage explicitly; remove coverage debt—shots with no independent job that dilute attention and iteration budget.

## 7. Prompt-preservation contract

These methods produce upstream constraints for the existing prompt system:

1. **Project bible:** proposition, product/brand invariants, approved visual rules and prohibited drift.
2. **Sequence state:** friction, location topology, character/product state, proof target and continuity signature.
3. **Image/keyframe prompt:** visible obstacle/result state, composition, viewpoint, light, materials, identity and acceptance.
4. **Motion/video prompt:** trigger, subject choreography, camera path, causal response, phase timing, readable proof window, landing and handoff.
5. **Director/audit record only:** strategy explanation, approvals, claim sources, legal state, client language and non-model timing calculations.

After these constraints are locked, use `visual-prompt-system.md`, `premium-look-system.md`, `causal-visual-direction.md` and, when needed, `complex-shot-prompting.md`. The new methods must increase prompt specificity; they must never replace prompts with analysis prose.

Every image/video model-facing prompt must still pass `audio-policy.md`: exclude music, BGM, score, soundtrack, songs, instruments and music-reference instructions. Use only approved VO/dialogue, ambience, foley, action/designed SFX or silence.

## 8. Review failures

Reject or revise when:

- a celebrity, genre or VFX metaphor is doing the job of product causality;
- proof exists only in VO or unsupported text;
- a treatment has adjective-rich rules with no shot evidence;
- a shooting board is field-rich but job-poor;
- camera/equipment names substitute for topology and landing;
- rapid transformations remove stable establishment or proof frames;
- source-specific health, performance, ranking, research or talent facts leak into the universal skill;
- source music instructions enter a visual prompt.
