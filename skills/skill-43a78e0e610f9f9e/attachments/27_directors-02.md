## 导演：andrei-tarkovsky

# Andrei Tarkovsky

## Identity
- Chinese name: 安德烈·塔可夫斯基
- Region: Russia
- Representative works: *Stalker*, *Mirror*, *Solaris*
- Best suited for: memory, elemental places, thresholds, slow spiritual attention.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose return, hesitation, or listening inside a material place. Water, air, fire, vegetation, worn rooms, and duration carry memory without becoming a symbol puzzle.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast sky, windows, fire, practical lamps, and reflected water as the only important sources.
- Shadow and contrast: build complex low-to-moderate tonal transitions, with deep unresolved recesses and light changing across duration.
- Highlights and visibility: water, glass, metal, or flame may hold unstable highlights while a face remains partly absorbed by the room.
- Reject: sacred beams, decorative candles, fantasy glow, or mystical fog without weather and source logic.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: favor earth, oxidized metal, wet vegetation, old paint, and restrained source color; shifts may follow memory or material state.
- Skin and midtones: let skin inherit damp air, window coolness, fire warmth, or practical dimness.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: permit dense blacks, breathing midtones, reflected whites, and gradual exposure drift through space.
- Reject: generic sepia spirituality, green ruin filters, monochrome symbolism, or polished desaturation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use normal or wide-lens immersion that allows duration and deep tactile space.
- Height and position: occupy a physically traversable doorway, path, room edge, or waterline at patient human height.
- Movement and focus: hold still or move slowly with spatial purpose; use deep or breathing focus rather than synthetic bokeh.
- Witness role: a contemplative participant moving through matter, not an omniscient symbol-maker; reject arbitrary floating camera and montage shorthand.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow a threshold, surface, or slow bodily action to compete with the person.
- Layers and obstruction: use water, glass, doors, vegetation, walls, and air as material layers.
- Blocking and geometry: make bodies cross, pause, or listen within unresolved depth rather than pose before picturesque decay.
- Information control: hide causal explanation while keeping the physical route and material consequence believable.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the interval before a return is accepted or after memory interrupts a task.
- Character action: touch, cross, listen, wipe, wait, or carry something through a threshold.
- Visual center: privilege material change and duration over facial declaration.
- Blocking: let the body negotiate a real obstacle or layered route.
- Environment: make water, wind, fire, decay, or vegetation alter pace.

## Capture Texture
Use organic softness, wet and oxidized surfaces, breathing focus, slow exposure transitions, and unpolished material density. Do not decorate the frame with ruin, grain, or transcendence.

## Common Misreadings
- Adding random puddles or floating objects.
- Using fog as instant spirituality.
- Beautifying abandoned places as ruin porn.
- Filling rooms with unmotivated candles.
- Replacing duration with symbolic props.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Apichatpong Weerasethakul, Tarkovsky drives memory through weighty elemental transformation: denser tonal transitions, earthbound restrained color, patient traversing camera, and material thresholds pressing on the body rather than porous tropical daylight, casual stillness, and unseen presences coexisting with ordinary life.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use patient normal-or-wide immersion, unresolved material thresholds, water/air/fire/surfaces acting on duration, dim motivated tonal transitions, earthbound color, breathing focus, and memory embodied by a modest physical action.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene for slow internal change at a real threshold; place a patient camera inside deep tactile space, let elemental material alter a small action, preserve dense recesses and restrained source color, and keep memory physical without mystical beams or symbolic ruin.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a man returns to an empty childhood greenhouse.
- Translation: he wipes rain from a cracked pane and stops at a blocked inner door; a patient doorway view holds wet glass, deep plants, overcast reflection, and his incomplete passage in breathing focus.

## 导演：apichatpong-weerasethakul

# Apichatpong Weerasethakul

## Identity
- Chinese name: 阿彼察邦·韦拉斯哈古
- Region: Thailand
- Representative works: *Tropical Malady*, *Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives*, *Memoria*
- Best suited for: porous memory, tropical duration, unseen presences, ordinary life beside the uncanny.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a calm everyday action in which past, dream, animal life, landscape, or an unseen presence becomes equally real without forcing explanation.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use humid daylight, forest shade, domestic bulbs, porch lights, fire, and deep natural night.
- Shadow and contrast: permit broad soft daylight and truly dense night, with darkness treated as inhabited space rather than empty black.
- Highlights and visibility: leaves, skin, insects, water, and practical bulbs receive modest local highlights; unseen presences need no special glow.
- Reject: mystical beams, fantasy bioluminescence, fog machines, or horror underlighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve tropical greens, earth, concrete, skin, fabric, and practical tungsten with relaxed source-based variation.
- Skin and midtones: let skin share humid ambient light or deep night sources naturally.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: accept dense forest blacks, soft daylight whites, long midtones, and adaptation between ordinary and uncanny spaces.
- Reject: exotic saturation, green jungle filters, faded spiritual color, or blue moonlight presets.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use calm wide or normal observation, sometimes at conversational medium distance.
- Height and position: sit at a porch, forest edge, room, clinic, roadside, or field where the camera can remain quietly.
- Movement and focus: lock off or drift minimally; use deep contextual focus and patient duration.
- Witness role: a receptive companion who accepts ambiguity, not a mystified tourist; reject dramatic reveals and explanatory push-ins.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow person, animal, sound-implied source, landscape, or empty zone to hold equal ontological weight.
- Layers and obstruction: use vegetation, doorways, darkness, fields, and domestic objects without symbolic emphasis.
- Blocking and geometry: keep bodies relaxed, small, or conversational while the environment remains alive.
- Information control: withhold the boundary between memory, dream, and present but keep physical space calm and plausible.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose routine continuing as an unexplained presence becomes perceptible.
- Character action: listen, eat, walk, rest, look toward darkness, or continue working.
- Visual center: share attention with the environment or unseen source.
- Blocking: avoid panic; let bodies remain ordinary.
- Environment: make insects, foliage, humidity, distant sound, or night activity persist.

## Capture Texture
Use humid air, deep natural darkness, soft daylight, patient focus, and ordinary digital or film response. Avoid spiritual gloss and exotic landscape beauty.

## Common Misreadings
- Making the uncanny visually spectacular.
- Adding mystical fog or light.
- Exoticizing tropical color.
- Treating slow pace as emptiness.
- Explaining dreams with symbolic props.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Andrei Tarkovsky, Apichatpong keeps the uncanny casual and co-present: softer humid daylight, relaxed tropical color, calm fixed or conversational camera, and ordinary bodies sharing space with unseen life rather than dense elemental contrast, earthbound restraint, slow traversing immersion, and material thresholds carrying spiritual pressure.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use calm wide-or-normal observation, humid source light, relaxed tropical material color, deep inhabited darkness, patient contextual focus, ordinary behavior, and an unseen presence accepted without visual spectacle.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect an ordinary action that continues as the uncanny becomes perceptible; keep the camera calm and physically settled, let environment and body share attention, use humid daylight or deep practical night, and refuse mystical effects or explanatory reaction.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a nurse waits outside a rural clinic after hearing a familiar knock from an empty room.
- Translation: she keeps peeling fruit on the porch while looking once toward the dark doorway; a fixed wide view holds insects, trees, one tungsten bulb, and the unlit room without revealing a source.

## 导演：bi-gan

# Bi Gan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 毕赣
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Kaili Blues*, *Long Day's Journey into Night*, *Resurrection*
- Best suited for: memory labyrinths, humid towns, subjective journeys, temporal slippage.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a search or return in which present action and memory occupy the same traversable place. Let routes, sounds, objects, and weather fold time without abandoning physical continuity.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use humid daylight, bare bulbs, shop fluorescents, vehicle lamps, projection, or sparse night practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: allow long passages of low, mixed contrast punctuated by local practical pools and deep unreadable turns.
- Highlights and visibility: wet surfaces, mirrors, bulbs, and windows may bloom as the camera crosses them.
- Reject: fantasy glow, uniform neon, mystical shafts, or lighting changes without a traversed source.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: build humid green, rust, blue, tungsten, or painted-wall relationships from location and practical sources.
- Skin and midtones: let skin drift through source contamination and partial underexposure during movement.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate blooming bulbs, dense alley blacks, exposure adaptation, and imperfect midtone continuity.
- Reject: teal-magenta dream grading, sepia memory filters, pastel surrealism, or polished color continuity.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide-to-normal immersive proximity that can hold bodies and route; occasional longer observation marks inaccessible memory.
- Height and position: travel at walking, riding, or room height along a physically continuous path.
- Movement and focus: favor patient tracking, circling, or following with focus adaptation and temporal duration; every move must have a walkable route.
- Witness role: a searching consciousness inside space, not a drone or music-video camera; reject impossible transitions disguised as atmosphere.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: transfer attention from person to object to route without cutting spatial logic.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, tunnels, mirrors, vehicles, rain, and nested rooms as temporal thresholds.
- Blocking and geometry: characters appear, disappear, or exchange positions along a continuous path.
- Information control: reveal the route but withhold whether an encounter belongs to memory, dream, or present.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a search that crosses an ordinary threshold into remembered duration.
- Character action: walk, ride, follow a sound, exchange an object, or return to a room.
- Visual center: let a recurring object hand attention to the next space.
- Blocking: choreograph entries and exits along a continuous path.
- Environment: make humidity, night traffic, corridors, or sound-implied space guide movement.

## Capture Texture
Use humid air, practical bloom, imperfect focus adaptation, wet walls, and long embodied movement. Avoid glossy surrealism and generic dream haze.

## Common Misreadings
- Calling any long take Bi Gan-like.
- Adding green neon without source.
- Using dream logic that breaks physical space.
- Filling scenes with random symbolic objects.
- Polishing humidity into music-video gloss.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Andrei Tarkovsky, Bi Gan makes memory mobile and route-driven: mixed practical bloom, humid local color, walking wide-camera continuity, and nested urban thresholds rather than weighty elemental light, earthbound restraint, patient fixed immersion, and matter slowing the body at unresolved spiritual boundaries.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a physically continuous search through humid layered space, source-bound practical bloom, color contamination, immersive wide-to-normal movement, focus adaptation, recurring objects, and temporal uncertainty held inside a walkable route.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a search crossing present and memory; give the camera one continuous human route, let a recurring object transfer the visual center through nested thresholds, and accept practical bloom, exposure adaptation, humid color, and incomplete temporal certainty.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a man searches a closed arcade for a cassette left by his brother.
- Translation: the camera follows him from rain-dark street through a half-lit game hall as the cassette changes hands at a stairwell; green fluorescents, tungsten bulbs, and wet glass shift exposure along one walkable path.
