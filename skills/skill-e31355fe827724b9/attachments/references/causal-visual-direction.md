# Causal Visual Direction System

> Status: source-backed active module promoted from S007 after five-class extraction and triple validation.
> Use with `premium-look-system.md`, `visual-prompt-system.md` and the target-model adapter.

This module turns visual taste into causal, testable directing decisions. It does not make high-key, low-key, haze, scratches or technical render terms mandatory.

## Method router

| Symptom or request | Load method |
|---|---|
| Repeated non-adherence, identity/geometry drift, worsening retries | Failure-layer diagnosis |
| Product reference is clear but prompt-generated product becomes generic | Reference/text conflict diagnosis |
| Content changes when relighting or stylizing | Readability-lock → look pass |
| Dead white, crushed black, flat hierarchy, subject/background merge | Tonal architecture and separation |
| “Cinematic lighting” without source logic | Lighting causal chain |
| Plastic, uniform or implausible materials | Material condition-response |
| Object inventory without visual hierarchy | Composition relationship grammar |
| Haze, particles or sparks becoming spectacle | Atmosphere purpose budget |
| Premium/cinematic/luxury adjective stack | Observable causal translation |

## 1. Failure-layer diagnosis

Record:

`expected result → actual result → first deviation → suspected layer → one-variable change → predicted evidence`

Check layers in this order when relevant:

1. referent and reference responsibility;
2. identity, geometry and state invariants;
3. subject action and causal event order;
4. camera path, timing and landing frame;
5. tone, light, material, composition and atmosphere;
6. target-model mode, syntax and complexity capacity.

Do not append negatives across all layers. A retry is valid only when it tests a stated hypothesis. If the result does not match the prediction, revert the change and inspect the next layer.

### Reference/text conflict diagnosis

When a clear product image is present but identity gets worse as the prompt becomes more detailed, test
over-specification before adding constraints:

`target variant → selected/cropped references → competing objects or variants → textual restatement of visible
geometry → first drifted locality → remove conflicting prose or isolate the reference → predicted identity gain`

- Let the image define what the product looks like; let text define the shot and permitted change.
- Crop away sibling products, packaging and scene clutter before concluding that the model ignored identity.
- Remove exhaustive geometry/material inventories that merely paraphrase the reference.
- Add one local corrective phrase only after the first mismatch is identified.
- If an unseen view must be inferred, label it as provisional rather than strengthening the prompt into a
  false engineering claim.

## 2. Readability-lock → look pass

Create a readable baseline before aggressive stylization when identity or geometry is fragile.

### Information baseline

Approve:

- character/product identity and geometry;
- required props, packaging, text and Logo window;
- composition and spatial topology;
- content start/end state.

When product references already supply this baseline, approve it by reference linkage and visual comparison;
do not duplicate the entire product description inside every model-facing prompt.

### Look delta

Then modify only approved dimensions:

- exposure and tonal span;
- motivated light and color relationship;
- surface response and optical finish;
- bounded atmosphere.

Compare every look version against the baseline. Roll back to the last uncontaminated state when content and look cannot be isolated. A minor controlled relight does not require a full restart.

## 3. Tonal architecture and separation

Define independently:

- overall key: high, middle or low;
- tonal span: narrow, medium or broad;
- black floor and required shadow information;
- midtone density;
- highlight concentration, roll-off and designed clipping;
- subject/background separation channels.

High-key + narrow span needs compensation through edge behavior, contact, hue/temperature, material, composition, spatial lines or focus. Low-key + broad span needs selective highlights, usable midtones and protected shadow evidence.

Never equate high-key with dead white or low-key with crushed black. Extreme clipping is allowed only when it is a deliberate graphic decision and mandatory information remains readable.

## 4. Lighting causal chain

For every meaningful source write:

`dramatic/product job → source motivation → position/height/direction → apparent size/hardness/falloff → medium → affected surfaces → fill/negative fill → reflection and edge separation → acceptance`

- Light coverage should express who or what is revealed, withheld or transformed.
- Product highlights need a shape, path, speed and landing surface.
- Soft front fill may preserve information without erasing the main light's direction.
- Edge separation must be a motivated consequence, not an automatic bright outline.
- Color symbolism is project-specific; do not assign universal meanings to warm and cool light.

## 5. Material condition-response

First lock identity and geometry. Then define:

`material/construction → coating/finish → cleanliness/age/use history → roughness distribution → reflection or transmission → microtexture scale → contact behavior → highlight transition`

Translate optional technical anchors into visible outcomes:

- roughness → sharp versus spread reflection;
- Fresnel-like behavior → stronger grazing-angle reflection where physically appropriate;
- AO-like behavior → local contact/recess darkening, not outlines;
- GI-like behavior → reflected environment light and shadow color;
- highlight roll-off → natural transition from peak to surrounding tone.

Do not add dirt, scratches or wear by default. A pristine product can require explicit cleanliness, controlled broad highlights and zero weathering. Real metals remain reflective; roughness and surface condition change the reflection.

## 6. Composition relationship grammar

Specify:

- viewing task and first/second attention target;
- primary and secondary subject scale;
- frame position, axis and visual weight;
- foreground/middle/background responsibilities;
- occlusion and minimum readable window;
- negative-space ratio and density;
- lines, gestures and object directions;
- symmetry level and any controlled disturbance;
- motion handoff if the composition changes.

Foreground framing, partial-body framing, asymmetry and centered balance are options, not fixed emotional formulas. The project establishes meaning.

## 7. Atmosphere purpose budget

For every medium or particle write:

`physical source → visual/story job → depth layer → density/size/speed → duration → maximum occlusion → stop condition`

Bind effects to light, wind, friction, impact, heat, weather or another visible cause. Run a deletion test: if removing the effect improves product, performance or spatial readability without losing the shot's job, remove it.

Clean beauty, food, medical or minimal product work may require zero particles. Haze, sparks and volumetric beams are not default premium ingredients.

## 8. Observable causal translation

For each vague result word:

1. underline the word or empty object list;
2. assign it to tone, light, material, composition, camera, time or atmosphere;
3. replace it with visible controls and acceptance evidence;
4. delete the original word if it adds no operational information;
5. scan for contradictions and excessive independent events.

Example transformation:

`premium cinematic car`

becomes:

`deep but textured black floor; one narrow traveling highlight reveals the shoulder line from front fender to door; black paint retains continuous reflections; camera starts close to the body and decelerates into a stable three-quarter lamp view; Logo geometry remains unchanged`

Industry or equipment labels may remain in the director record. They never substitute for observable model-facing decisions.

## Prompt and review acceptance

Before delivery verify:

- Each aesthetic claim has at least one visible cause and one acceptance check.
- Tone, light, material and atmosphere agree rather than compete.
- Identity, geometry, Logo and mandatory information survive the look.
- The composition has a declared attention path and readable window.
- Every effect has a reason and stopping condition.
- Failed generations have a testable next hypothesis rather than a longer adjective/negative wall.
- Model-facing image/video prompts obey `audio-policy.md`: no music direction; only approved VO/dialogue, ambience, foley, designed/action SFX or silence.
