# Short-Drama White Soft-Light Look

## Default visual target

Use a polished Chinese short-drama beauty look unless the user or accepted continuity state explicitly requires another treatment.

The target is not a generic beauty filter. It is a controlled photographic look:
- bright, clean, flattering facial exposure;
- large diffused key/fill impression;
- soft transitions across forehead, cheeks, nose, jaw, and neck;
- luminous midtones and gentle highlight roll-off;
- low-to-moderate microcontrast on skin while retaining eyes, eyelashes, lips, hair, fabric, and props;
- subtle white diffusion, bloom, and halation around practical highlights;
- restrained skin smoothing with visible pores and facial structure;
- clear catchlights and emotionally readable eyes;
- stable skin tone across cuts and profiles.

## Practical visual language

Use wording such as:
- `短剧白柔光质感`
- `大面积柔和主光与轻柔补光`
- `面部阴影过渡柔和，眼神光清晰`
- `中间调明亮通透，高光柔和滚降`
- `轻微白色扩散与低强度辉光`
- `皮肤干净细腻但保留毛孔和骨相`
- `背景保持空间层次，不做奶雾化`

When a photographic analogy helps, use restrained wording such as `1/8级柔光滤镜观感` rather than asserting a physical filter was used.

## Exposure and skin rules

- Keep faces approximately 0.3-0.7 stops brighter than the immediate environment when narratively appropriate.
- Preserve forehead, nose bridge, cheekbone, and white wardrobe highlight detail.
- Keep skin neutral-warm unless the scene requires a cold emotional bias.
- Separate skin from similarly colored walls or costumes through soft luminance and hue contrast.
- Preserve natural lip color and eye whites; do not bleach or gray them.
- Avoid over-sharpened pores, hard nasolabial shadows, hollow eye sockets, and harsh under-chin shadows.

## Day interiors

- Favor window-softbox impression, high-key fill, clean neutral whites, and gentle warm skin.
- Keep background whites slightly below facial highlights to avoid a flat white wall effect.
- Use subtle depth separation through falloff, not heavy blur alone.

## Night interiors

- Maintain the scene's practical-light color while adding an invisible soft frontal or three-quarter fill impression.
- Keep faces readable and flattering without making the room look daytime bright.
- Control neon and lamp bloom so it does not veil facial features.

## Historical and costume short drama

- Preserve costume texture, embroidery, hair ornaments, and makeup detail.
- Use soft frontal/three-quarter keying, luminous skin, restrained warm highlights, and elegant low-contrast facial rendering.
- Do not turn the image into modern studio glamour when the scene requires candlelight, moonlight, rain, smoke, or battlefield atmosphere.

## Suspense, conflict, and tragedy

- Keep the white soft-light skin treatment but allow deeper environmental contrast and cooler shadows.
- Retain facial readability and eye light while preserving danger, fatigue, rain, smoke, or grief.
- Do not erase bruises, tears, dirt, sweat, or story-relevant texture.

## Continuity lock

For S02+ inherit:
- facial luminance relative to background;
- skin warmth and saturation;
- diffusion strength;
- bloom radius and intensity;
- microcontrast and sharpening;
- black level and midtone lift;
- catchlight size and direction.

At the continuation boundary, prohibit sudden changes in whitening, smoothing, bloom, haze, skin exposure, or facial contrast.

## Hard avoidances

Avoid:
- waxy or plastic skin;
- porcelain-white bleaching;
- clipped forehead, nose, or cheek highlights;
- gray, bloodless, or magenta skin;
- heavy milk fog across the whole frame;
- excessive bloom that obscures eyes and lips;
- flat lighting with no facial structure;
- inconsistent beauty strength between cuts;
- over-sharpened skin after a soft shot;
- AI-style face replacement blur or identity drift.

## S02+ continuation-clip priority

For S02+, do not re-impose a generic beauty preset. Measure the white-soft-light treatment from the accepted continuation clip's final stable 1-2 seconds and inherit:
- facial luminance relative to the background;
- skin warmth, saturation, and whitening strength;
- diffusion strength and bloom radius;
- catchlight size and direction;
- facial microcontrast and smoothing;
- black level, midtone lift, grain, and sharpening.

The continuation clip overrides a conflicting generic short-drama look or separate style card at the boundary. The new segment may refine only after a motivated lighting change shown in the timeline; it may not reset the beauty treatment at 00:00 or after a cut.
