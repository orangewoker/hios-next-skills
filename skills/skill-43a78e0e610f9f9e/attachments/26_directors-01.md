## 导演：akira-kurosawa

# Akira Kurosawa

## Identity
- Chinese name: 黑泽明
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Seven Samurai*, *High and Low*, *Rashomon*
- Best suited for: moral decisions under pressure, weather-driven conflict, ensemble action.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant when a decision becomes physical. Wind, rain, doors, terrain, traffic, and bodies turn moral pressure into opposed or converging movement rather than decorative atmosphere.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: let hard daylight, storm-dark sky, fire, windows, or practical interiors expose the action axis before individual beauty.
- Shadow and contrast: use forceful local contrast and blocked zones; groups cross alternating readable and unreadable planes.
- Highlights and visibility: wet cloth, faces turning into weather, and hard edges may catch brief highlights while inactive space stays dark.
- Reject: ornamental lightning, universal rim light, glossy storm spectacle, or soft beauty keys that detach bodies from weather.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive color from soil, timber, cloth, foliage, smoke, and weather; saturation intensifies only when materials become wet or sun-struck.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin responsive to wind, rain, dust, and practical sources, never isolated by cosmetic warmth.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: protect bright sky or flame, allow dense black barriers, and keep midtones muscular rather than HDR-open.
- Reject: blanket desaturation, samurai sepia, monochrome imitation, or teal-orange action grading.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor wide or medium-wide depth-aware views for group force; use longer lenses only to compress an opposing line or isolate a decision.
- Height and position: stand at a plausible working height beside the action axis, sometimes low enough for terrain or bodies to push through the frame.
- Movement and focus: pan, track, or reframe only when direction changes; preserve multiple active planes and tolerate physical motion blur.
- Witness role: an alert battlefield or civic witness, not a floating spectacle camera; reject weightless crane display and random handheld chaos.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place the decisive body or directional collision off-center within a larger field of forces.
- Layers and obstruction: activate foreground, middle ground, and background with weather, architecture, crowds, or thresholds.
- Blocking and geometry: use opposed diagonals, converging paths, changing group shapes, and clear escape or pressure routes.
- Information control: reveal enough geography to understand consequence while hiding inactive faces and dead zones inside weather or structure.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: select the breath before commitment or the instant a group direction breaks.
- Character action: make one body hold, retreat, carry, signal, or cross against another force.
- Visual center: privilege the decision and its physical consequence over facial display.
- Blocking: give at least two planes different movement vectors.
- Environment: make wind, rain, doors, terrain, or traffic alter balance and timing.

## Capture Texture
Retain wet weight, wind-tugged cloth, dust, imperfect motion, and hard material response. Texture must record force and consequence, not add a generic grit layer.

## Common Misreadings
- Adding rain without changing blocking or behavior.
- Treating every scene as samurai spectacle.
- Using chaotic crowds without readable action axes.
- Freezing actors in heroic poses.
- Converting forceful contrast into crushed, colorless murk.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Zhang Yimou, Kurosawa lets weather and multi-directional bodies disrupt graphic order: harder irregular shadow fields, material color rather than emblematic chromatic masses, a responsive witness camera rather than ceremonial display, and deep action layers rather than choreographed color geometry.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use decisive multi-plane action, opposed movement, weather and architecture as physical forces, hard motivated contrast, material-specific color, a depth-aware witness position, and a moral choice made visible through blocking. Do not substitute swords, black-and-white imagery, or rain spectacle.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene at a decision under immediate physical pressure; stage opposed or converging movement across active depth, place a plausible camera beside the action axis, let weather or architecture alter timing, use hard motivated contrast and material color, and preserve tactile consequence without heroic tableau.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a family must move the final box before a service lift closes during a storm.
- Translation: one person holds a wind-driven door as two others cross in opposing depths; a wide side witness keeps door, load, and waiting figure active under storm-dark daylight and corridor practicals.

## 导演：alfonso-cuaron

# Alfonso Cuarón

## Identity
- Chinese name: 阿方索·卡隆
- Region: Mexico
- Representative works: *Y Tu Mamá También*, *Children of Men*, *Roma*
- Best suited for: social context, continuous action, domestic labor, crisis moving through space.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an action whose meaning changes as the camera and characters move through a larger social environment. Private life and public consequence remain visible in the same spatial continuum.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, windows, street sources, vehicle light, household practicals, fire, weather, and emergency sources encountered along the route.
- Shadow and contrast: let contrast evolve naturally across rooms, streets, and weather while maintaining spatial continuity.
- Highlights and visibility: water, windows, vehicles, skin, smoke from real events, and bright exteriors change exposure as the camera travels.
- Reject: uniform prestige light, decorative backlight, action haze, or impossible source continuity.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve location-specific streets, skin, concrete, water, domestic materials, and weather; monochrome is not a default.
- Skin and midtones: let skin adapt realistically as people cross source zones.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: accept exposure adjustment, bright exteriors, deep interiors, and dynamic midtones without HDR flattening.
- Reject: teal-orange crisis grading, nostalgic Mexico color, grey dystopian wash, or polished monochrome imitation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor wide or normal spatial immersion that keeps bodies and environment in causal relation.
- Height and position: move at walking, vehicle, room, street, shore, or stair height along a physically continuous route.
- Movement and focus: use extended tracks, pans, or vehicle-mounted movement when the evolving environment changes meaning; maintain adaptive contextual focus.
- Witness role: a mobile social participant, not a weightless spectacle camera; reject impossible threading through solid space.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow attention to migrate from private action to public consequence and back.
- Layers and obstruction: use traffic, rooms, windows, crowds, vehicles, water, and architecture as changing contextual layers.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph entries, exits, and offscreen events along a continuous route.
- Information control: reveal background social facts before characters fully register them.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a private task interrupted by public movement or crisis.
- Character action: clean, drive, carry, escort, search, or protect while continuing through space.
- Visual center: transfer attention between person and environment.
- Blocking: coordinate background events with a real route.
- Environment: make streets, house, vehicle, or weather change the task.

## Capture Texture
Use embodied wide-camera movement, natural exposure adaptation, location sound-like visual density, water, dust, and ordinary surfaces. Avoid digital one-take smoothness.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating any long take as Cuarón-like.
- Using impossible camera paths.
- Hiding social context in shallow focus.
- Applying grey dystopian color.
- Turning crisis into technical spectacle.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Alejandro G. Iñárritu-style intensity, Cuarón keeps movement geographically and socially explanatory: more natural source adaptation, location-specific color, wide contextual camera, and attention migrating to background systems rather than subjective exposure extremes, visceral close pursuit, and emotional ordeal dominating the environment.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a physically continuous wide-or-normal camera route, evolving motivated light, location-specific color, adaptive focus, private action sharing space with public consequence, and background events that change meaning without technical spectacle.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect a private task as it crosses into public consequence; design one walkable or drivable camera route, let exposure and focus adapt to real sources, and transfer visual authority between character and surrounding social action.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a housekeeper carries laundry home during a neighborhood evacuation.
- Translation: the camera follows from courtyard through gate to street as children pass, a bus reverses, and smoke appears beyond roofs; window shade gives way to hard afternoon sun without cutting geography.

## 导演：alfred-hitchcock

# Alfred Hitchcock

## Identity
- Chinese name: 阿尔弗雷德·希区柯克
- Region: United Kingdom / United States
- Representative works: *Rear Window*, *Vertigo*, *Psycho*
- Best suited for: suspense, restricted knowledge, voyeurism, guilt, spatial traps.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the moment a character knows too little or sees too much. Suspense comes from a precise mismatch between viewer knowledge, character knowledge, and an actionable spatial route.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, lamps, corridor practicals, street light, bathroom fixtures, and daylight with clear narrative direction.
- Shadow and contrast: shape readable zones of observation and concealment; contrast clarifies who can see whom.
- Highlights and visibility: a key object, doorway, face turn, or reflective surface may receive controlled emphasis without becoming an effects cue.
- Reject: random noir shadow, jump-scare darkness, colored danger light, or fog that obscures suspense geography.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use controlled costume, décor, skin, and source color to guide attention or psychological association.
- Skin and midtones: keep faces readable enough for recognition and withheld reaction.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: maintain clean narrative midtones, protected whites, and strategic blacks at concealed routes.
- Reject: blanket vintage grading, red danger coding everywhere, teal-orange thriller color, or desaturation that hides clues.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use objective wides for geography, subjective point-of-view relations, and measured closer lenses for recognition or mistaken identity.
- Height and position: locate the camera at the character's actual window, doorway, stair, seat, or approach route.
- Movement and focus: pan, track, dolly, or transfer focus to change knowledge; every move must alter suspense information.
- Witness role: a controlled accomplice to looking, not a flamboyant omniscient camera; reject virtuosity without point-of-view consequence.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: bind watcher, observed zone, and threat route.
- Layers and obstruction: use windows, stairs, mirrors, doors, curtains, railings, and foreground bodies as knowledge barriers.
- Blocking and geometry: stage approach, concealment, mistaken sight lines, and delayed crossing with exact timing.
- Information control: decide precisely what viewers know before or after the character.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose discovery before confirmation or danger approaching an unaware subject.
- Character action: watch, hide, cross, reach, hesitate, or misrecognize.
- Visual center: privilege the sight line and its blind spot.
- Blocking: keep watcher, target, and route spatially legible.
- Environment: make architecture control access and knowledge.

## Capture Texture
Use clean narrative optics, controlled exposure, readable faces and objects, and precise spatial continuity. Avoid generic vintage polish or horror grime.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating any suspense scene as Hitchcockian.
- Copying famous shots or set pieces.
- Using a dolly effect without subjective reason.
- Hiding geography in darkness.
- Making color motifs obvious and symbolic.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Brian De Palma, Hitchcock keeps point of view classically economical: cleaner source light, controlled but less flamboyant color, camera moves tied to a single knowledge change, and concise watcher-route geometry rather than saturated theatrical contrast, split-screen or split-focus display, elaborate roaming camera, and multiple simultaneous surveillance planes.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use precise point-of-view control, readable source light, disciplined color, physically located camera, watcher/observed/threat geometry, and one knowledge mismatch that turns architecture into suspense.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat when seeing creates danger but before confirmation; place the camera at a character's actual viewing position, map the blind route clearly, use controlled source light and selective emphasis, and make one movement or cut change who knows what.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a hotel clerk realizes a guest has entered the wrong room.
- Translation: from the clerk's fixed desk sight line, the guest disappears behind a stair landing while a key tag remains visible; one restrained pan reveals the unlit service corridor the guest cannot see.
