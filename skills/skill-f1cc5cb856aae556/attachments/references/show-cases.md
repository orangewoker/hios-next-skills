# Show Cases（双模式各≥1 条【优质】）

成片 Skill 硬性验收：≥2 条【优质】成片范例。以下线稿、彩稿各一【优质】，加一条线稿备选。

---

## Show Case 1【优质】· 线稿模式 · 六翼炽天使

**主题**：天野流天使设定稿态（白底墨线、未完成优雅）
**情绪曲线**：静滞→微动→凝视→羽化消散
**视觉符号**：翼（镜1初现、镜4呼应）、光环（镜3强化）

**成片 prompt（镜3 凝视定格，可直接出图/图生视频首帧）**：
```
Everything breathes the same breath — the figure, the six wings, the gold ring, the
drifting motes — all rendered in hair-thin lines that dissolve at the edges, as if the
whole scene were a half-remembered dream.
Amano-style angel line draft, a six-winged seraph suspended in white air,
androgynous face with a razored pointed chin, long narrow eyes, oversized pupils 70% of
each eye gazing past the viewer into a serene void,
limbs elongated, floating without gravity,
long flowing hair in a few decisive sweeping ink lines, sharing the same floating breath
as the wings, ends dissolving, never a solid mass,
wings as a few decisive radiating long lines, suggested by overlapping hair-thin strokes,
the same breath as the body,
a delicate gold line circle as halo — intensified at gaze, the same breath as the wings,
hair-thin black ink brush lines with breathing weight, contours unclosed, dissolving into
white,
70% of the frame untouched white mulberry paper, alive with faint silver motes and a
crescent moon upper left,
no shading, no cast shadow — light emanates from within,
asymmetrical — halo center-upper, figure lower-right 30%,
ink, silver leaf, on mulberry paper,
line draft, sketch-like, unfinished elegance, ethereal, solemn divine void, 2.39:1
```

**分镜（5 镜 / 约 21s）**：
1. 静滞 4s：天使悬浮白中，翼几根线初现，留白吞没。
2. 微动 4s：发/翼/衣袂同向微浮，气线相连。
3. 凝视 5s：光环金圈第二次强化，眼神与画外静默对望（定格）。
4. 华满 4s：翼线最满展，仍是同一种呼吸。
5. 羽化 4s：线尾渐溶入白，翼呼应镜1同一根线，留白吞没主体。

## Show Case 2【优质】· 完成彩稿模式 · 古典女性（黑底金线）

**主题**：天野 1990s 成熟期画集完稿态（黑底金线、铅笔稿可见、淡彩罩染、华丽神性，近 Princess Budu / 源氏物语古典女性）
**情绪曲线**：华美展开→凝滞神性→余韵
**视觉符号**：新月冠/额饰（镜1初现、镜末呼应）、长发金线（贯穿）、淡粉花瓣（点缀）

**成片 prompt（镜2 凝滞神性，可直接出图/图生视频首帧）**：
```
Everything breathes the same breath — the woman, her crescent crown, her drifting hair, the
falling petals — all rendered in hair-thin gold-and-ink lines over a visible pencil sketch,
as if the whole scene were a half-remembered dream held in black silk.
Amano-style 1990s mature-period illustration in the manner of Katen / Biten / Princess Budu,
a classical androgynous woman in three-quarter profile, face turned slightly away,
thin gaunt face with a razored pointed chin, long narrow eyes with lowered lids, oversized
vacant pupils gazing downward and past the viewer, an expression of cold aloofness touched
with sadness, never looking directly at the camera,
elongated neck and limbs, floating without gravity, no anatomical weight,
hair as a few decisive long gold-and-ink lines that drift and dissolve at the ends, sharing
one floating breath with the robe and the crown, never a solid mass,
a slender crescent crown and a single forehead jewel woven into the hair with the same thin
gold lines,
layered ornamental robes in ivory white and pale beige, suggested by gold thread and a few
decisive strokes, Byzantine-meets-Heian decorative motifs — circles, vines, petals,
traditional illustration: colored inks and matte gold leaf layered over a visible pencil
underdrawing, re-inked with wispy black line, on a deep pure black ground,
the black ink line is the dominant statement; color is a translucent watercolor wash laid
over it, the deep black ground showing through,
matte gold leaf, no glossy highlight, no glowing bloom,
flat decorative clarity — no volumetric shading, no soft 3d skin, no cast shadow,
no chiaroscuro; the figure is ghost-like and translucent, mass and texture dissolved into
phantom light,
hair-thin lines with broken, breathing weight, dry-brush fragments and breaks, not smooth
uniform strokes, contours unclosed,
restrained color palette — deep black, matte gold, ivory white, pale beige, with only a few
blush-pink petals drifting in the void, no vermilion, no indigo, no violet,
asymmetrical — figure right 35%, the left 65% pure dissolving black void with faint gold
particles and a thin silver crescent moon,
ornate, classical, divine, cold fantasy, ethereal, matte, restrained palette, 2.39:1 cinematic ratio
```

**分镜（5 镜 / 约 22s）**：
1. 华美展开 5s：黑底上金线/装饰逐层浮现，长发金线初现。
2. 凝滞神性 5s：人物定格，神性疏离达顶点（定格）。
3. 华满 4s：珠宝/额饰最满，与发线同呼吸。
4. 微动 4s：衣袂/发尾同向缓浮。
5. 余韵 4s：金箔微光退散，头冠呼应镜1，留一丝冷艳。

## Show Case 3（备选·线稿）· 妖精

**主题**：天野流妖精线描态（白底、花冠、月牙替代光环）
**prompt（镜1）**：
```
Everything breathes the same breath — the fairy, the flower crown, the crescent, the
falling petals — all rendered in hair-thin lines that dissolve at the edges.
Amano-style fairy line draft, an androgynous fairy with a flower crown,
razored pointed chin, oversized vacant pupils gazing past the viewer,
long flowing hair in a few decisive lines, sharing the same floating breath as the wings,
tiny line-wings suggested by a few strokes, a thin crescent moon instead of halo,
hair-thin black ink lines with breathing weight, contours unclosed, dissolving into white,
70% of frame untouched white mulberry paper, falling pale petals dissolving into white,
no shading, light from within, asymmetrical — figure left 35%,
ink, gold leaf, watercolor on mulberry paper,
line draft, unfinished elegance, ethereal, innocent but non-human, 2.39:1
```
