Evidence-grade options must be defined by the project and distinguish at least:

- verified released/implemented capability;
- verified prototype or research-stage demonstration;
- dramatized reconstruction;
- illustrative inference/analogy;
- brand declaration.

## Generator boundary

- Model prompts may contain only approved visible behaviour and states.
- Do not make a research concept appear released, an enactment appear documentary, or a brand declaration appear measured proof.
- Generate legal text/Super as controlled post graphics unless the chosen workflow reliably preserves exact text.
