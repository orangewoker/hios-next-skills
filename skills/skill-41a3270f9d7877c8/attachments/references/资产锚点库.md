# 资产锚点库（成片四资产）

成片 Skill 的 4 类复用资产：角色锚点 / 场景锚点 / 道具锚点 / 分镜锚点。复制即用。

---

## 一、角色锚点（按子类型）

| 子类型 | 面部 / 神情 | 头发 | 和服 / 装饰 | 色系锚点 |
|--------|-----------|------|------------|----------|
| 狐妖 | `razored pointed chin, vacant half-lidded gaze, eerie elegant smirk not cute` | `long dark hair, few decisive lines, ends dissolve` | `flowing red-and-indigo kimono, gold-thread motifs, fox-mask in hand` | 狐妖朱金 |
| 源氏女性 | `thin gaunt face, lowered lids, sorrowful noble detachment` | `elaborate updo with long escaping lines, gold pins` | `layered Heian robe, vermilion and indigo, gold leaf` | 源氏金朱 |
| 妖精 | `androgynous, vacant pupils, non-human innocence` | `petal-like few lines` | `light kimono, ultramarine and viridian wash, gold line` | 妖精群青 |
| 画皮式 | `pale ghostly face, hollow calm gaze, haunting beauty` | `long pale hair, few lines` | `pale robe with blood-red accent at collar / plum` | 画皮苍白 |

**人物统一锚点（全身竖版，全子类型必含）**：
```
full-body vertical portrait, the figure occupying most of the frame,
elongated neck and limbs, floating without gravity,
a few decisive long lines for hair that dissolve at the ends,
flowing kimono with swaying sleeves and obi, suggested by gold-thin strokes,
never a solid mass, never a cute anime face
```

## 二、场景锚点（淡线描环境）

| 环境元素 | 淡线描表达（通透不填实心） |
|----------|---------------------------|
| 鸟居 | `a torii gate suggested by a few pale gold lines — two pillars and one crossbeam, hollow, no solid fill` |
| 神社 / 鸟居林 | `faint torii silhouettes in the distance, hair-thin lines dissolving into void` |
| 月 | `a thin gold circle moon, Klimt-like, not a glowing mass` |
| 樱枝 | `a few cherry branches as delicate ink lines with sparse petals` |
| 水波 | `stylized water ripples as concentric thin gold lines` |
| 远山 | `a faint outline of a mountain, one breathing stroke` |

**环境气连锚点**：`vast empty space alive with faint gold lines, so the emptiness breathes with the figure`

## 三、道具锚点

| 道具 | 淡线描 / 彩稿表达 |
|------|------------------|
| 和服衣袂 | `sleeves and obi as few sweeping lines, floating with the same breath` |
| 发饰 | `slender gold pins / kanzashi as a few decisive gold lines` |
| 扇 | `a folding fan suggested by thin radiating lines` |
| 狐面具 | `a fox mask held in hand, a few pale lines, not a furry head` |
| 提灯 | `a paper lantern as a thin gold-outlined oval with faint glow` |

## 四、分镜锚点（8 列模板见 分镜与prompt模板.md）

每个成片建议 5–8 镜，单镜 3–10s，情绪曲线贯穿。视觉主符号（和服衣袂 / 长发 / 鸟居 / 月）重复≥2 次且首尾呼应。默认竖版 1024×1792 出静帧，视频按 2.39:1 横版或竖版适配。
