# 部件卡6 · Wind anchor

## 固定头部
`Wind anchor全段持续:` 室外/开放环境必写，室内封闭环境声明"无风"。

## 室外正确
```
Wind anchor全段持续: continuous low night wind hum with fabric rustling in the breeze
持续低频夜风，布料在风中细微摆动
```
万物生原版（对照用）：
```
Wind anchor全段持续: continuous low desert wind hum with high-frequency sand particle hiss,
visible sand streamers being lifted off the dune ridges and trailing laterally across the air
throughout the frame, fabric edges and hair strands moving in the breeze.
```

## 室内无风正确
```
Wind anchor全段持续: no wind no air movement in the enclosed chamber, absolute stillness
throughout, no fabric movement no hair movement
室内封闭空间完全无风，全段衣料与发丝绝对静止
```
❌ 括号格式非标准：「(本段为室内环境，无持续风环境态)」

## 要点
- Wind anchor写"风声+可见效果"（沙粒横向飘过/布料头发飘动），不只写声音
- 室内无风也要写，防止AI默认加风
