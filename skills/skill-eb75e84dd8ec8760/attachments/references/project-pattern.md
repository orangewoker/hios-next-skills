# Validated project pattern: warehouse green-peppercorn livestream

## Style definition

This is a fixed-phone warehouse ecommerce livestream, not iPhone selfie UGC and not polished broadcast-studio photography. The useful look is a believable phone livestream: 9:16, eye-level or slightly low fixed position, practical warehouse lighting, modest depth, restrained movement, ordinary working props, and a presenter speaking directly to the lens.

## 原始五段口播母版

默认把以下句式视为锁定母版。只替换花括号中的变量；不得自行调整句序、口语节奏、价格揭示方式或行动号召。

### 第一段：产品钩子与购买门槛

> 特{感官卖点一}特{感官卖点二}的{产品名}，全部都是{批次或新鲜度卖点}。{不适用人群或购买门槛}千万别拍。

青麻椒原版：

> 特麻特香的青麻椒，全部都是今年刚摘的新货。不能吃麻的千万别拍。

### 第二段：市场对比与第一次报底价

> 线下多少钱{计价单位}，我就不多说了，你们可以随便去搜。今天给你们发{成交规格}啊，{对比价}都不做。给我改价，{成交价}！给我上链接！

青麻椒原版：

> 线下多少钱一斤，我就不多说了，你们可以随便去搜。今天给你们发一斤啊，十九都不做。给我改价，七块九毛钱！给我上链接！

保留画外场控节奏：“给我改价”后回应“好”；“给我上链接”后回应“上链接”。如果用户明确取消场控，才删除这些画外音。

### 第三段：规格与价值证明

> 我今天直接给你发整整{成交规格}，实打实的，都是{产品名}。就做{成交价}。

青麻椒原版：

> 我今天直接给你发整整一斤重，实打实的，都是青麻椒。就做七块九毛钱。

保留画外场控回应“太合适了”，除非用户明确取消。

### 第四段：品质、用途与效果证明

> 全部都是{批次或新鲜度卖点}，{品质排除项}，{核心气味或口感卖点}，品质特别好。拿回去{用途一}、{用途二}、{用途三}，{用量或使用动作}，{使用结果卖点}。买{计价规格}都老贵了，

青麻椒原版：

> 全部都是今年新货，不掺陈货，麻香味浓郁，品质特别好。拿回去做水煮鱼、麻辣火锅、卤肉爆炒，丢一小把，满屋子飘香。买一斤都老贵了，

### 第五段：活动理由、稀缺性与行动号召

> 今天要不是{活动原因}，这样的价格你根本买不到。福利不多，抢到就是你的。关键真的划算！来，就{成交价}到手{成交规格}！给我去拍！

青麻椒原版：

> 今天要不是厂家搞活动，这样的价格你根本买不到。福利不多，抢到就是你的。关键真的划算！来，就七块九毛钱到手一斤！给我去拍！

## 母版继承规则

1. 默认沿用五段结构和每段原有职责，不重新写一套“更像广告”的文案。
2. 固定文本一字不改；只填写经过用户确认的变量槽位。
3. 同一变量必须全片一致，例如成交规格和成交价不得在不同段落漂移。
4. 如果新产品不适合某一槽位，先指出事实冲突，再提出最小替换，不得静默改写整句。
5. 先确认变量表，再确认替换后的五段完整文案；两次确认不能合并。
6. 用户如果提供了新的母版，以用户明确指定的新母版为准，但同样执行固定文本与变量槽位锁定。

## Assets that formed continuity anchors

- Role: young Chinese woman, white short-sleeve qipao, blue butterfly embroidery, dark frog closures, high bun, small white floral hair ornaments.
- Scene: tall blue-and-orange warehouse shelving, cardboard cartons, central aisle, white table, fixed phone at the outer edge, casually placed walkie-talkie.
- Product: clear zip bag of green Sichuan peppercorns, recognizable bag proportion, believable fill level, irregular natural granules.
- Spatial continuity: presenter centered in the same aisle; table and two bags preserve scale; phone and walkie-talkie imply a working livestream rather than set dressing.

## What worked

- Segment 1 establishes origin quickly through a shelf-to-camera action.
- Segments 2–5 retain a stable table axis, making hard cuts feel continuous.
- Exact product count in prompts reduces duplication and disappearance.
- Gesture-linked copy makes price and quantity claims easier to follow.
- Segment 4 gives a distinct proof beat instead of repeating the sealed-bag shot.

## Risks found during review

- Transparent packaging can warp or change size when gripped, rotated, or held horizontally.
- Peppercorns can become uniform pellets if particle texture and scale are not locked.
- Opening and resealing a zipper bag is a complex hand-object interaction and should be isolated.
- Repeated push-in/pull-out directions contradict a fixed-phone livestream and can look synthetic.
- An offscreen response can accidentally create an extra visible person unless explicitly forbidden.
- Generic adjectives such as “热情” are weak unless tied to a precise gesture, gaze, or beat.
- Automatically generated captions, music, logos, or package text can damage realism.

## Generalization rules

1. Replace the product but preserve the selling functions.
2. Keep role, scene, product, pose, and voice as separate references with explicit duties.
3. Use a reusable person-free, product-agnostic scene asset.
4. Design one fragile interaction per segment at most.
5. End each accepted segment in a clean state that can support either a tail continuation or hard cut.
6. Treat a close product display as its own proof beat, with a dedicated approved composition when necessary.
7. Prefer modest phone-camera realism over cinematic camera choreography.
