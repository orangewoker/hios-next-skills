# 东方天宫镜头图谱

Use this atlas to select a deliberate visual family. Do not blend several families unless the user explicitly asks for a maximal collage; choose one primary grammar and let any secondary element support it. The one controlled exception is an axial path plus a side spectacle: the path remains the master geometry and the spectacle stays subordinate in one side sector.

## Shot families

| ID / family | Image grammar | Figures | Typical motion for image-to-video |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ceiling-gaze` 天地柱与藻井仰视 | 18–24mm, extremely low interior angle; giant carved columns and caisson ceiling crop out of frame, vertical pressure; a narrow sky opening | One scale token walking at the base | Barely perceptible low upward tilt |
| `side-colonnade` 无尽侧廊 | 20–28mm oblique lateral view; repeating columns/eaves/railings converge diagonally; cloud sea opens on one side | One or two scale tokens or ceremonial witnesses | Slow lateral drift along the colonnade |
| `threshold-reveal` 阈限框景 | Stand inside a dark hall; beams, eaves, gate, or columns create a frame within a frame; reveal an immense outer world | 1–4 quiet figures at the threshold | Very slow push toward/near the frame |
| `ascension-axis` 登天轴线 | 24–35mm low central view; monumental stairs, bridge, or ramp lead to a huge distant palace; symmetry is intentional | Two scale tokens or a sparse procession | Slow forward climb/push; no lateral drift |
| `remote-compression` 远距压缩 | 70–100mm telephoto; tiny foreground platform/rail/pine/pavilion, immense far palace still occupies much sky through multiple cloud layers | 1–3 tiny figures | Almost locked off or slow pull-back |
| `curved-canopy` 环形天幕 | 18–28mm wide; curved eaves, ring roof, or sweeping colonnade arcs around an open sky/cloud void | 1–2 ceremonial witnesses at the curve | Slow lateral arc or nearly locked shot |
| `architecture-landscape` 建筑即地貌 | 28–50mm; palace fuses with cliff, floating rock, waterfall wall, or enormous structural escarpment; use a partial architectural foreground | Small group placed on terrace/platform | Slow push toward the landscape relation |
| `cosmic-portal` 月门与宇宙门户 | 35–50mm; one huge circular gate/arch frames moon, planet, far pagoda, or cloud world; axial framing is allowed | One isolated scale token | Almost locked-off; moonlight/haze changes only |
| `spectacle-observatory` 奇观观察台 | 24–35mm from palace walkway/balcony; viewers observe one anomaly such as cloud whale, sky waterfall, or floating pine | 1–2 readable ceremonial witnesses | Locked-off or subtle push; anomaly moves naturally |

## Controlled composite: axis plus side spectacle

Use this only when the reference visibly combines an axial skyway/stair/door with one dramatic event at the side.

- Keep `ascension-axis` as the primary family: one central skyway, stair, gate, or luminous door controls the vanishing system and destination.
- Put the single spectacle in the left or right third, never across the axis or in front of the destination. It may occupy visual area, but the path must remain readable from foreground to door.
- Use one ceremonial witness on the start platform or two small scale tokens on the axis. Let the figure face away toward the destination; do not add a separate viewing crowd.
- Use only one effect source: one whale breach and one spray zone, one waterfall source, or one cloud-tide edge. Fog may veil the giant palace, but its roof mass and main opening must remain readable.

## Figure modes

Choose exactly one mode. Percentage values are visual targets, not a calculation instruction.

| Mode | Use it for | Prompt constraints |
|---|---|---|
| `scale-token` 尺度 token | Pure monumental scale, distant palace, endless corridor, colossal interior | 1–3 people, roughly 0.3–0.8% of frame height, far away, back/side-facing, low contrast, no face detail |
| `ceremonial-witness` 仪式性见证者 | Emotional pauses, balcony viewpoints, red-gold light, portals, paired figures | 1–2 people, roughly 3–8% of frame height, lower edge or side, backs/silhouettes, readable robe/dragging hem/reflection, still no face close-up |
| `inhabited-procession` 栖居行列 | A lived-in heaven road, ritual passage, large open hall | 12–40 small dots/groups spaced across depth; a few nearer figures may be readable but no dense crowd or faces |

## Light and material modes

| Mode | Palette and light | Architectural emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| `aerial-jade` 晴空白玉 | Clear blue sky, blue-white cloud sea, restrained warm rim light | White jade, pale stone, cloud reflection, dark timber only as accent |
| `lacquer-gold` 朱红金光 | Low-angle amber side/reverse light against cool shadows | Cinnabar lacquered columns, black/brown timber, gold caisson, wet stone reflections |
| `bronze-dusk` 铜黑暮色 | Bronze dusk, deep shadow, warm lanterns, smoke/haze | Old wood, bronze lamps, weathered stone, dense interior depth |
| `moon-portal` 月夜灰蓝 | Cool moonlight or silvery blue, a restrained warm edge inside | Circular stone gates, dark red timber, cloud mist, far pagoda/planet silhouette |
| `silhouette-veil` 雾幕逆光 | Blue-gray mist and low warm back/side light; a glowing doorway or thin rim separates the temple from fog | Dark layered roof mass, silhouetted columns, one luminous aperture, pale stone skyway |

## Hero anomaly palette

Pick no more than one for a frame. Put it in a clear spatial relation to the architecture and people.

| Anomaly | Make it credible by | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `sky-waterfall` 天瀑 | Fix its source at a hall edge, cliff, or aperture; let spray blend into cloud layers | Multiple unrelated waterfalls |
| `cloud-whale` 云海鲸 | Place witnesses on a railing or at the start of a central skyway; make the whale emerge from the cloud ocean with realistic wet surface and one splash zone, without blocking the master path | Extra mythical animals, a tiny decorative whale, or a second breach |
| `cliff-palace` 崖壁天宫 | Build terraces, roofs, or gates into one massive geological/structural wall | Turning every surface into random rocks |
| `moon-arch` 月门天体 | Use one circular opening/arch to frame a moon/planet and distant tower | Adding extra portals, suns, or planets |
| `pine-gate` 古松与天门 | Let one ancient pine physically cross/frame a gate or pavilion | Garden courtyard, bonsai, or pine in every shot |
| `cloud-tide` 云潮天阶 | Make cloud spill like a tide along a clear stair or platform edge | Replacing all solid architecture with clouds |

## Series diversity grid

For a five-shot set, choose one usable example rather than repeating a favorite frame:

| Beat | Suggested family | Typical light / figure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Establishing scale | `remote-compression` or `architecture-landscape` | `aerial-jade`, `scale-token` |
| Passage | `side-colonnade` or `ascension-axis` | `lacquer-gold`, `scale-token` or `inhabited-procession` |
| Revelation | `cosmic-portal` or `spectacle-observatory` | `moon-portal` or `bronze-dusk`, `ceremonial-witness` |
| Breath/detail | `ceiling-gaze` or `curved-canopy` | Any unused light mode, usually `scale-token` |
| Departure | `threshold-reveal` | Return to the established world palette, `ceremonial-witness` or `scale-token` |

## Motion locks by family

Use one camera action only. Motion should reveal existing depth, not manufacture new geometry.

- `ceiling-gaze`: low upward tilt; columns/caisson do not bend or grow.
- `side-colonnade`: slow lateral drift; repeated bays preserve their spacing.
- `threshold-reveal`: very slow push toward/through the opening; distant subject remains fixed in scale.
- `ascension-axis`: slow forward push up the axis; stairs and rails stay straight/symmetrical.
- `remote-compression`: almost locked-off or very slow pull-back; haze drifts without shrinking the far palace.
- `curved-canopy`: subtle lateral arc; roof curvature stays rigid.
- `architecture-landscape`: slow push; waterfall/mist only moves at environmental speed.
- `cosmic-portal`: locked-off; moon/planet, arch, and tower never drift relative to each other.
- `spectacle-observatory`: locked-off or minimal push; only the existing whale/water/pine receives natural micro-motion.
- `axis-plus-side-spectacle`: slow forward push on the existing axis only; the whale or water event continues one existing natural arc, spray settles into cloud, and the glowing destination remains fixed.

## Quick selection rules

- Choose symmetry for pilgrimage, stairway, gate, or cosmic portal; choose asymmetry for discovery, corridor rhythm, and terrace observation.
- For a central path plus one side spectacle, keep symmetry in the structural geometry and asymmetry only in the anomaly placement; never add a second destination, path, or anomaly.
- Choose `scale-token` when architecture must feel crushingly large; choose `ceremonial-witness` when emotion needs readable robes and a quiet social relationship.
- Keep world continuity with materials, costume palette, cloud height, and light direction. Create variety through shot geometry, not random extra decoration.
