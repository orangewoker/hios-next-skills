## Category cautions

- **Food, cosmetics, pet products:** do not invent ingredients, clinical effects, nutrition, safety, or certifications. “Conceptual visualization” must be labeled when depicting absorption, protection, filtration, or internal effects.
- **Electronics:** do not invent ports, battery capacity, charging speed, waterproofing, sensors, or internal components.
- **Soft goods:** keep weave scale, seam count, zipper direction, and deformation physically plausible.
- **Transparent containers:** preserve liquid level, refraction, label wrap, and cap geometry across shots.
- **Variant films:** lock everything except the feature that truly changes.

## Example 15-second structures

### Earbuds

`0–3.5s mesh/ring clue → 3.5–8s hinge and magnetic choreography → 8–12s material/fit proof → 12–15s open-case hero`

### Phone case

`0–4s edge-on white contour → 4–9s texture/button/camera-lip macros → 9–12s phone insertion only with supplied compatible device → 12–15s color hero`

### Pet treat pack

`0–3s pack edge as architecture → 3–8s truthful texture/opening/portion → 8–12s one supplied proof claim → 12–15s pack-and-product lockup`

The premium language comes from restraint and evidence, not from making every category look metallic or futuristic.
