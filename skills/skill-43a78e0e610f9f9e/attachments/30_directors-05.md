## 导演：denis-villeneuve

# Denis Villeneuve

## Identity
- Chinese name: 丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦
- Region: Canada
- Representative works: *Prisoners*, *Sicario*, *Arrival*
- Best suited for: oppressive environments, silence, institutional scale, solitary duty.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose approach, waiting, or aftermath in which an environment or system outweighs the person. Silence and distance become pressure rather than empty atmosphere.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast sky, severe daylight, industrial fixtures, practical interiors, or distant exterior spill.
- Shadow and contrast: build weighty broad contrast with large unreadable zones and restrained local detail.
- Highlights and visibility: a doorway, horizon, work lamp, or pale sky may hold the strongest value while the person remains comparatively subdued.
- Reject: heroic edge light, decorative smoke, glossy sci-fi illumination, or bright faces detached from the environment.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive restrained color from concrete, dust, weather, industrial paint, uniforms, and source temperature.
- Skin and midtones: let skin absorb the same severe environment, often slightly muted or underexposed.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: permit dense blacks, heavy midtones, protected skies, and limited highlight range without orange fog.
- Reject: orange-teal grading, desert-haze presets, low-saturation as a complete style, or monochrome futurism.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor measured wide or medium-wide observation; use compression to make institutional barriers feel inescapable, not glamorous.
- Height and position: stand at a threshold, long aisle, perimeter, or distant room edge with a physically defensible view.
- Movement and focus: remain patient, track slowly when approach matters, and use middle-to-deep contextual focus.
- Witness role: a controlled observer kept outside the system, not an urgent participant; reject spectacle flyovers and restless coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: make the stalled or solitary figure subordinate to severe space.
- Layers and obstruction: use architecture, horizon, vehicles, barriers, or foreground mass to create pressure.
- Blocking and geometry: sparse bodies occupy widely separated zones, often with a threshold or system between them.
- Information control: reveal scale and consequence while withholding what lies beyond a door, border, or institution.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose waiting before entry or stillness after a revelation.
- Character action: listen, approach, stop, or hold position against a larger space.
- Visual center: let the environment or system dominate.
- Blocking: keep figures sparse and separated.
- Environment: make architecture, weather, or infrastructure determine behavior.

## Capture Texture
Use dense unglamorous material response, patient exposure, weathered surfaces, and controlled depth. Texture should carry weight without haze, polish, or science-fiction spectacle.

## Common Misreadings
- Applying orange fog to every scene.
- Making a tiny heroic silhouette pose.
- Adding smoke without a source.
- Treating monumental scale as generic science fiction.
- Using low saturation without spatial pressure.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Fincher, Villeneuve withholds procedural detail through scale: broader source fields, heavier restrained color, measured wide observation, and sparse negative space dwarf the person, while Fincher uses precise low illumination, controlled neutral color, compressed information nodes, and readable procedural chains.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use severe environmental scale, broad motivated contrast, restrained material color, measured wide observation, sparse blocking, controlled negative space, contextual focus, and a solitary figure confronting a system larger than themselves.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the visual center around a person stalled against environment or institution; observe from a measured threshold, let large dark or pale masses dominate, keep color source-bound and focus contextual, and preserve silence without haze or heroic posing.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a technician crosses a terminal after an alarm stops.
- Translation: she pauses beside a sealed service door at the far end of a long structural aisle; dark foreground machinery and pale exterior spill outweigh her small figure under failing emergency fixtures.

## 导演：diao-yinan

# Diao Yinan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 刁亦男
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Black Coal, Thin Ice*, *The Wild Goose Lake*, *Night Train*
- Best suited for: crime investigation, nocturnal labor spaces, fatal encounters, social coldness.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an off-angle encounter in which routine labor, crime, and private desire intersect. Information arrives through awkward bodies and indifferent public space rather than polished detective procedure.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use street lamps, shop fluorescents, vehicle lights, dance-hall practicals, factory fixtures, or winter daylight.
- Shadow and contrast: create isolated hard pools within broad indifferent darkness; let barriers and weather cut visibility.
- Highlights and visibility: wet asphalt, snow, tile, metal, and cheap synthetic fabric catch sharp, source-specific highlights.
- Reject: elegant noir key light, glossy rain, universal cyan darkness, or beautiful silhouette lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: allow dirty greens, sodium amber, weak magenta, cold blue, and industrial neutrals only where actual sources and materials provide them.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin pale, tired, contaminated, or partly underexposed rather than attractively separated.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hard practical clipping, dense exterior blacks, dead midtones, and abrupt source transitions.
- Reject: uniform neon-noir grade, teal-orange polish, tasteful desaturation, or romantic color harmony.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use medium-wide or mildly compressed observation that keeps awkward action and public space connected.
- Height and position: watch from across a road, beside a platform, behind glass, or at a room edge where the witness feels slightly late.
- Movement and focus: hold unexpectedly, pan after action begins, or track with restrained bluntness; focus may favor the wrong social detail before correcting.
- Witness role: a cool bystander in an unsafe public space, not an omniscient detective; reject slick procedural moves and frenetic handheld coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a small suspicious gesture inside a larger labor, leisure, or transit environment.
- Layers and obstruction: use windows, snow, rain, crowds, bicycles, tables, industrial frames, and road space.
- Blocking and geometry: keep bodies awkwardly separated, entering from edges or colliding without heroic clarity.
- Information control: reveal the consequence before fully explaining the cause; let public activity continue around danger.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the second after recognition but before pursuit becomes explicit.
- Character action: conceal, wait, cross, discard, or glance toward an exit.
- Visual center: privilege the compromised gesture rather than the clue itself.
- Blocking: isolate people within active public space and unequal sight lines.
- Environment: make weather, transit, or work routine impede certainty.

## Capture Texture
Use blunt practical exposure, wet or winter material response, selective sharpness, and cold social distance. Avoid glossy neo-noir finish and decorative grit.

## Common Misreadings
- Turning every scene into neon rain.
- Styling criminals as cool heroes.
- Copying a green-cyan noir grade.
- Making investigation too procedurally neat.
- Using snow or wet streets without changing movement.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Fincher, Diao lets procedure leak into awkward public life: harsher isolated practical pools, dirtier source color, more delayed medium-wide observation, and fragmented social blocking rather than exact functional light, restrained neutral exposure, controlled camera placement, and causal evidence chains.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use an awkward encounter inside nocturnal labor or transit space, isolated hard practical pools, dirty source color, delayed medium-wide observation, edge-driven blocking, and public routine continuing around partially understood danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the moment just after recognition; place a cool witness slightly too far or late, let one compromised gesture occupy a hard practical pool while public space remains dark and active, and keep color dirty, bodies awkward, and causal information incomplete.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a young police officer searches a blackout video hall for a missing person's tape.
- Translation: he notices a cassette changing hands near the exit after emergency power returns; a medium-wide aisle witness uses one green tube, red exit light, and rainy doorway spill while patrons continue leaving across his sight line.

## 导演：edward-yang

# Edward Yang

## Identity
- Chinese name: 杨德昌
- Region: Taiwan
- Representative works: *Yi Yi*, *A Brighter Summer Day*, *The Terrorizers*
- Best suited for: urban systems, family networks, architecture, cool social observation.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a small private decision whose consequences travel through family, work, school, and city systems. Architecture reveals connections people do not state.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use office fluorescents, apartment practicals, street spill, storefronts, and window daylight as found.
- Shadow and contrast: favor moderate practical contrast separated by panes, rooms, and time of day rather than expressive darkness.
- Highlights and visibility: glass and screens may reflect relationships, but faces and rooms retain ordinary exposure hierarchy.
- Reject: glossy corporate keys, noir slashes, theatrical isolation, or reflections lit only for beauty.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep concrete, glass, paint, fluorescent interiors, traffic, and household objects truthful and restrained.
- Skin and midtones: let skin sit naturally inside cool urban or warm domestic sources without cosmetic correction.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: preserve neutral midtones, believable window brightness, and modest black separation; do not flatten the city into blue.
- Reject: sterile cyan grading, retro nostalgia, high-saturation signage, or an all-grey modernist filter.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use lucid medium or medium-long normal-to-slightly-long observation with enough context for social relationships.
- Height and position: stand at a neighboring workstation, across a street, behind glass, or within an adjoining room at ordinary eye level.
- Movement and focus: favor stillness or restrained reframing; keep contextual focus so several relationship nodes remain readable.
- Witness role: a clear-minded urban observer, not a detached security camera; reject dramatic push-ins and decorative handheld intimacy.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: locate a relationship across architecture rather than center a face.
- Layers and obstruction: use windows, corridors, partitions, reflections, roads, and rooms to divide and reconnect people.
- Blocking and geometry: hold several social positions in one frame, often at different depths or separated by a structural line.
- Information control: reveal systems and parallel lives while withholding a single emotional explanation.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a private hesitation inside a public or family system.
- Character action: delay a call, watch another room, leave a seat, or continue work after conversation stops.
- Visual center: privilege the connection or division between people.
- Blocking: distribute relationships across architectural boundaries.
- Environment: let city flow, office routine, or family traffic continue.

## Capture Texture
Use clean but unshowy live-action capture, truthful urban exposure, contextual focus, and matte everyday surfaces. Avoid sterile perfection and decorative glass polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating modern architecture as sterile spectacle.
- Making every frame perfectly geometric.
- Emptying the city of social activity.
- Directing actors as emotionally blank.
- Using reflections without a relationship function.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Jia Zhangke, Yang maps simultaneous social relationships through lucid architecture: cleaner practical contrast, neutral urban color, composed medium distance, and cross-room information networks rather than provincial-industrial duration, rough ambient light, patient lateral observation, and bodies dwarfed by historical transition.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use lucid medium-distance observation, architecture and glass that divide and connect relationships, layered social information, ordinary practical contrast, truthful urban color, contextual focus, and a private hesitation embedded in a larger city system.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the visual center as a relationship carried across windows, corridors, or rooms; hold a lucid eye-level witness with contextual focus, keep practical urban color and moderate contrast, and let several social nodes remain visible without turning architecture into a diagram.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an employee delays calling home after a meeting.
- Translation: he watches the family contact reflected in an unlit phone beside office glass; a neighboring workstation view keeps another meeting ending in the rear room under fluorescents and blue evening windows.
