## 导演：christopher-nolan

# Christopher Nolan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 克里斯托弗·诺兰
- Region: United Kingdom / United States
- Representative works: *Inception*, *Dunkirk*, *Oppenheimer*
- Best suited for: time pressure, mechanisms, intersecting tasks, physical stakes.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant when a clock, route, machine, weather event, or limited space forces a concrete choice. Link one human action to a larger causal event.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, weather, dashboard glow, work lights, fire, emergency fixtures, or machinery already present.
- Shadow and contrast: favor robust practical contrast that keeps routes, mechanisms, and consequences readable.
- Highlights and visibility: protect bright exterior, flame, or instrument highlights while faces move through functional shadow.
- Reject: abstract energy glow, decorative blue backlight, weightless explosion light, or darkness that hides geography.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use neutral, material-specific color with source-led warm/cool divisions between environments or timelines.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin human inside harsh practical conditions without glossy heroic correction.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold dense but readable blacks, protected highlights, and firm midtones that convey scale and weight.
- Reject: generic blue-grey blockbuster grade, orange-teal spectacle, metallic desaturation, or IMAX-like polish as a color rule.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor grounded medium-wide scale and context; use closer lenses only when a task's physical detail carries consequence.
- Height and position: stand beside a route, platform, cockpit, shoreline, or mechanism where geography can be sketched.
- Movement and focus: track, pan, or mount the camera to clarify intersecting action; keep contextual depth and crisp causal focus.
- Witness role: an urgent participant who understands mechanics, not an omniscient effects camera; reject impossible fly-throughs and empty handheld intensity.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: connect a hand, decision, or mechanism to a larger route or event.
- Layers and obstruction: use vehicles, machinery, architecture, weather, and crowds as causal planes.
- Blocking and geometry: cross action lines while preserving readable geography and deadlines.
- Information control: withhold outcome, not spatial cause; viewers should understand what can fail.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the second before commitment or during an irreversible task.
- Character action: check, turn, cross, release, signal, or hold a mechanism.
- Visual center: bind individual choice to system consequence.
- Blocking: organize intersecting routes or separated teams.
- Environment: make time, weather, or machinery materially constrain action.

## Capture Texture
Preserve weight, impact, vibration, weather, and practical exposure with clear material response. Avoid effects polish, artificial grit, and abstract time graphics.

## Common Misreadings
- Adding clocks without a physical deadline.
- Using explosions without causal stakes.
- Folding architecture as a visual token.
- Applying a generic blue-grey blockbuster grade.
- Making scale weightless or geography unreadable.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Denis Villeneuve, Nolan organizes pressure through readable mechanism and intersecting action: firmer practical contrast, neutral causal color, participant camera movement, and connected routes rather than oppressive stillness, restrained material color, measured distant observation, and environment withholding information.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a physical deadline, readable spatial causality, intersecting action lines, grounded medium-wide scale, practical contrast, neutral material color, contextual focus, and tactile consequence connecting one choice to a larger event.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the frame around a physical mechanism under immediate time pressure; place the camera beside the operative route, keep intersecting actions geographically clear, use practical light and firm exposure, and show weight and consequence without blockbuster color shorthand.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an engineer has one minute to restart a floodgate.
- Translation: she checks a wet gauge before turning the wheel; a shoulder-height platform witness keeps wheel, rising water, and a second worker's route legible under rain-dark daylight and functional indicators.

## 导演：david-fincher

# David Fincher

## Identity
- Chinese name: 大卫·芬奇
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Se7en*, *Zodiac*, *The Social Network*
- Best suited for: investigation, procedure, controlled interiors, uneasy information.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose verification, correction, comparison, or a process delayed by one missing fact. Tension comes from controlled observation straining against fatigue and incomplete evidence.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use office fixtures, task lamps, street spill, projector or screen-adjacent light, and practical windows with explicit functions.
- Shadow and contrast: allow low illumination only when the operative face, hand, paper, or object remains readable; secondary space falls into controlled shadow.
- Highlights and visibility: contain highlights on information surfaces and matte edges rather than beautifying skin.
- Reject: glossy noir slashes, universal darkness, green crime glow, or dramatic light that destroys evidence hierarchy.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use restrained neutral-to-cool material response with localized source contamination, never a blanket cast.
- Skin and midtones: keep tired skin credible inside practical light, neither orange nor waxy.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: control blacks without crushing narrative detail; hold paper and screen whites below sterile clipping; keep disciplined midtones.
- Reject: yellow-green filter, teal-orange crime grade, silver desaturation, or saturated hacker neon.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use exact medium or mildly compressed observation that connects evidence, action, and response.
- Height and position: choose one precise doorway, desk edge, interview axis, or work-surface height for an explicit information purpose.
- Movement and focus: lock off or adjust minimally when a new relationship must be revealed; focus organizes information nodes while retaining procedure context.
- Witness role: an exact analytical observer, not surveillance for its own sake; reject arbitrary intimacy, handheld agitation, and music-video movement.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: organize a chain from cause to evidence to response.
- Layers and obstruction: use doors, tables, lists, files, keys, and boxes as causal structure, not decoration.
- Blocking and geometry: place hands, faces, and objects so checking behavior is readable across controlled axes.
- Information control: make one or two evidence nodes fully legible and deliberately suppress secondary clutter.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose verification just before certainty or correction after an error.
- Character action: compare, mark, rewind, file, count, or pause over an inconsistency.
- Visual center: privilege the operative information chain.
- Blocking: align cause, evidence, and reaction without perfect graphic display.
- Environment: make work surfaces and institutional objects carry procedure.

## Capture Texture
Use clean matte surfaces, disciplined shadow detail, selective legibility, restrained sharpness, and fatigue in skin and materials. Avoid glossy crime-drama polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Applying a yellow-green filter.
- Making every room extremely dark.
- Decorating ordinary work with serial-killer grime.
- Using aggressive camera moves.
- Keeping every clue and surface equally sharp.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Denis Villeneuve, Fincher reduces space to a readable procedural chain: localized functional light, neutral controlled exposure, exact medium or compressed camera placement, and information-node composition rather than broad oppressive light, distant scale, sparse bodies, and environment-led negative space.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use exact procedural observation, functional low illumination, restrained neutral material color, disciplined focus, selective evidence legibility, controlled camera placement, and tension between visible order and internal fatigue.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a verification interrupted by one inconsistency; place a precise camera where cause, evidence, and response form a readable chain, expose for operative paper or hands, suppress secondary clutter, and keep matte neutral materials without a green crime filter.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a tenant performs one final check before returning an apartment key.
- Translation: she compares a handwritten list with a marked wall and places the key beside it; a doorway-height medium witness keeps hand, list, key, and mark connected under one ceiling practical and weak window spill.

## 导演：david-lynch

# David Lynch

## Identity
- Chinese name: 大卫·林奇
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Blue Velvet*, *Mulholland Drive*, *Lost Highway*
- Best suited for: fractured identity, domestic uncanny, dream logic, hidden violence.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an ordinary gesture that becomes emotionally or causally wrong. Let sound-implied space, repetition, performance, and abrupt tonal shifts expose a second reality without explaining it.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use domestic lamps, stage light, street lamps, headlights, windows, television, or industrial practicals with psychologically loaded placement.
- Shadow and contrast: permit velvet blacks, isolated pools, sudden overexposure, and calm daylight that turns wrong through context.
- Highlights and visibility: skin, curtains, polished wood, smoke, road surfaces, and faces may glow or disappear with unsettling precision.
- Reject: generic horror darkness, random colored bulbs, fog-only surrealism, or constant noir.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use deep red, blue, cream, green, night black, or suburban daylight only when tied to rooms, costume, stage, or identity.
- Skin and midtones: allow skin to become too pale, warm, flat, or shadowed according to the scene's reality shift.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: embrace dense blacks, theatrical whites, unstable midtones, and abrupt but motivated exposure changes.
- Reject: purple dream filters, vintage Americana wash, teal-orange neo-noir, or symbolic color pasted onto every frame.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use calm wide or normal observation, invasive close relation, or long-lens nocturnal detachment according to subjective threat.
- Height and position: occupy living rooms, stages, corridors, roads, clubs, or impossible-feeling but physically locatable corners.
- Movement and focus: hold too long, creep slowly, pivot, or cut across discontinuity; focus may isolate a banal object with causal uncertainty.
- Witness role: a subjective consciousness caught between realities, not an explanatory horror camera; reject jump-scare moves.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a banal object, performance, face, or dark zone displace ordinary narrative priority.
- Layers and obstruction: use curtains, doors, mirrors, stages, hallways, televisions, and night roads as reality seams.
- Blocking and geometry: repeat gestures, double identities, or place bodies too formally inside familiar rooms.
- Information control: show emotional truth while withholding literal causality.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant routine repeats incorrectly or a performance exposes another identity.
- Character action: listen, smile, rehearse, open, wait, or repeat.
- Visual center: privilege the wrong object or spatial seam.
- Blocking: use uncanny stillness, doubling, or repeated paths.
- Environment: make domestic or performance space conceal another reality.

## Capture Texture
Use deep source-bound blacks, practical glow, ordinary daylight, tactile curtains and interiors, and patient optical unease. Avoid generic dream haze and horror polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Calling random surreal imagery Lynchian.
- Using red curtains in every scene.
- Applying a purple or blue dream filter.
- Explaining the uncanny with symbols.
- Relying on jump scares or monster reveals.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Robert Eggers, Lynch destabilizes identity through subjective tonal discontinuity: more theatrical source pools, saturated psychological color, creeping or dislocated camera, and domestic/performance seams rather than period-authentic low light, earthbound restricted color, severe wide or normal observation, and bodies trapped by historical belief and elemental space.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use an ordinary gesture becoming causally wrong, psychologically loaded practical light, selective saturated room color, patient or creeping camera, a banal object or spatial seam as visual center, and emotional truth without literal explanation.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat when routine repeats incorrectly; hold or creep from a physically locatable but subjectively uneasy position, let one practical pool or daylight zone become wrong through behavior, and transfer attention to a banal object or doorway without surreal decoration.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a woman records a birthday greeting alone in a suburban kitchen.
- Translation: after stopping the camera she hears her own greeting continue from the dark living room; a fixed doorway view holds cream daylight, one red appliance light, and her repeated smile reflected in the blank television.
