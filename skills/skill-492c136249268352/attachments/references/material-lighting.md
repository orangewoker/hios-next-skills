# Material and lighting recipes

## Contents

1. Lighting controls

## 1. Lighting controls

Describe light with physical intent:

- source size: pin, narrow strip, medium softbox, large overhead diffusion;
- direction: grazing, back rim, top rake, frontal soft, underlight;
- hardness and falloff;
- color temperature or hue relationship;
- travel path and speed;
- what surface evidence the light must reveal.

Use one key behavior, one separation behavior, and only the fill required to preserve truthful color.

## 2. Material recipes

| Material | Key light | Supporting light | Camera/angle | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brushed metal | narrow grazing strip perpendicular to grain | cool edge separation | 70–100 mm, shallow angle | random grain, liquid chrome |
| Anodized aluminum | large soft source for smooth tone | thin opposite rim | 50–85 mm, controlled turn | blown edges, mirror finish |
| Matte plastic | large lateral softbox | negative fill for silhouette | 50–85 mm, moderate depth | oily speculars, chalky black |
| Gloss plastic | long strip reflection | soft frontal fill | 70–100 mm, angle for clean bands | chaotic room reflections |
| Glass | hard edge/backlight plus dark flags | tiny frontal lift | 85–120 mm, edge-on and 3/4 | white clipping, invisible thickness |
| Frosted/translucent | broad backlight through material | gentle rim | 70–100 mm | fake neon core unless intentional |
| Silicone/rubber | broad soft side light | low-level rim | 50–100 mm macro | metallic sheen, plastic hardness |
| Woven fabric | raking side light across weave | soft ambient fill | 90–120 mm macro | moiré, changing weave scale |
| Leather | large warm side key | narrow cool or neutral rim | 85–120 mm macro | exaggerated pores, wet gloss |
| Paper/cardboard | top-side soft key | subtle shadow control | 50–85 mm | crushed edges, plastic reflections |
| Ceramic | large curved reflection | small contour light | 70–100 mm | metallic sparkle |
| Liquid/gel | controlled backlight | polarizing-style clean highlights | macro/telephoto | impossible viscosity, extra droplets |
| Food/pet food | large soft appetizing key | warm edge and selective specular | 70–100 mm macro | plastic texture, false ingredients |

## 3. Color handling

- State the exact product color before mood grading.
- Keep key product color near neutral white balance unless the concept is explicitly chromatic.
- Use background colors derived from the product, complementary accents, or neutral black/white/gray.
- Avoid teal-orange grading by default.
- For black products, separate layers with luminance steps and edge lights rather than lifting black into gray.
- For white products, use negative fill and slight background luminance separation rather than gray outlines.
- For multiple variants, lock camera, lighting, and scale; change only color/finish unless the models truly differ.

## 4. Background decisions

| Product condition | Recommended field |
|---|---|
| dark glossy object | black void with controlled strips, or mid-gray for contour safety |
| pale matte object | white sweep with negative fill, or a 5–15% darker tone-on-tone field |
| translucent object | luminous gradient or backlit white field |
| textured soft good | muted product-color field with grazing light |
| many accessories | orthographic white/gray field for legibility |
| dramatic single hero | black void or one-color gradient chamber |

Use true void when weight is irrelevant. Use infinite sweep with micro contact shadow when physical trust and scale matter.
