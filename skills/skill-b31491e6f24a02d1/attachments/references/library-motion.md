# 资料库：运镜、调度、视角、焦点、剪辑

### @MOVEMENT：运镜体系


| 运镜 | 情绪与叙事 | AI执行锚点 |

|------|-----------|-----------|

| 固定镜头 | 客观、冷静、压抑 | `static tripod shot, absolutely no camera movement` |

| 固定+呼吸手持 | 临场感、纪录片真实感 | `subtle handheld breathing motion, barely perceptible micro-shake` |

| 缓慢推进 | 凝视加重，压力倍增 | `slow push-in, smooth dolly forward, intensifying emotional pressure` |

| 急速推近 | 世界崩塌，瞬间震惊 | `fast crash zoom, rapid dolly in with motion blur on edges` |

| 后拉 | 揭示环境，孤独无力 | `slow pull-back dolly out, revealing vast empty space around isolated subject` |

| 横移 | 跟随、揭示、探索 | `smooth tracking shot, lateral dolly, foreground elements occasionally wipe frame` |

| 上摇 | 揭示、仰望、压迫感 | `slow tilt-up from feet to face, revealing movement with judgment or awe` |

| 下摇 | 发现、降临、失落 | `tilt-down, sense of discovery or descent` |

| 甩摇 | 动能转场 | `whip pan transition, fast blurred horizontal pan, kinetic energy` |

| 斯坦尼康 | 沉浸式体验，冷静观察 | `steadicam floating shot, fluid and weightless, gliding through space` |

| 手持摄影 | 混乱、紧张、现实 | `shaky handheld camera, documentary-style realism, chaotic and disorienting` |

| 希区柯克变焦 | 眩晕、世界观崩塌 | `dolly zoom, vertigo effect, camera dollies forward while lens zooms out` |

| 旋转镜头 | 规则崩坏，精神错乱 | `barrel roll axial rotation, world literally turning upside down` |

| 浮动镜头 | 催眠感、灵魂出窍 | `ethereal floating camera, underwater weightlessness, slow-motion drift` |

| 脉冲式手持 | 力量碰撞、心脏悸动 | `pulse-reactive handheld shake, camera physically recoils with percussive impact` |

| 窥视式运镜 | 主观偷窥、恐惧 | `peeping camera movement, hidden behind blurry foreground, hesitant slow pan out` |

| 伪一镜到底 | 时间不可逆，命运无情 | `seamless pseudo-long take, camera weaves through crowd, passing behind pillar` |

| 360°环绕 | 主体被包围感、眩晕 | `360-degree orbital shot, camera circles around subject, sense of entrapment` |

| 俯冲镜头 | 从天而降的压迫感 | `dive-down shot, camera plunges from high angle to low angle` |
