## 导演：stanley-kubrick

# Stanley Kubrick

## Identity
- Chinese name: 斯坦利·库布里克
- Region: United States / United Kingdom
- Representative works: *2001: A Space Odyssey*, *The Shining*, *Barry Lyndon*
- Best suited for: ritual, institutional systems, geometry, controlled unease.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose routine becoming psychologically charged. Formal order makes a person's unstable position inside an institution, procedure, or room legible.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use credible practical, daylight, period lamps, or institutional fixtures arranged with formal consistency.
- Shadow and contrast: keep contrast disciplined and structural; broad lit planes meet deliberate recesses rather than expressionistic chaos.
- Highlights and visibility: practicals and architectural surfaces may be exact and bright while human faces remain coolly integrated.
- Reject: automatic horror red, decorative spotlighting, glossy sci-fi glow, or arbitrary high contrast.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use restrained but exact color relationships tied to production space, uniforms, décor, and source temperature.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin controlled within the room's formal palette rather than warming it for empathy.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: protect formal whites and lamp highlights, retain measured blacks, and avoid HDR expansion.
- Reject: sterile blue filter, copied red motifs, period sepia, or color coding without spatial function.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use deliberate wide or normal perspective that makes spatial geometry and body placement undeniable.
- Height and position: align the camera with a real architectural axis at a measured standing, seated, or corridor height.
- Movement and focus: use locked precision, controlled tracking, or slow axial movement; keep measured depth and avoid casual focus breathing.
- Witness role: an impersonal institutional observer, not a playful decorator; reject arbitrary extreme wide distortion and restless coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place human instability against order; centered framing is optional, not mandatory.
- Layers and obstruction: architecture, repeated fixtures, furniture, and thresholds define pressure with minimal clutter.
- Blocking and geometry: ritualize gestures and distances, using repetition, frontal planes, or central perspective only when the location supports them.
- Information control: expose the system clearly while leaving motive and emotion coolly unresolved.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a rule being followed one beat too long or a routine that starts to fail.
- Character action: align, wait, repeat, inspect, or violate a small procedure.
- Visual center: make order compete with one human irregularity.
- Blocking: use measured spacing and controlled axes.
- Environment: let architecture and repetition enforce behavior.

## Capture Texture
Use clean, materially credible capture, disciplined exposure, stable depth, and exact surfaces without digital gloss. Precision should feel coldly observed, not synthetic.

## Common Misreadings
- Centering every subject automatically.
- Copying one-point corridor perspective.
- Turning all scenes into horror.
- Treating actors as emotionless mannequins.
- Using extreme wide angles or sterile sci-fi as shortcuts.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Wes Anderson, Kubrick uses order as existential and institutional pressure: cooler formal source light, restrained exact color, psychologically distancing lens geometry, and ritualized bodies threatened by the system rather than storybook palette, lateral dollhouse staging, playful flatness, and handcrafted visual wit.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use deliberate wide-or-normal perspective, formal motivated light, restrained exact color, measured depth, ritualized blocking, controlled architecture, and visual order strained by one unstable human action. Do not default to symmetry.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a routine becoming unsettling; align a physically possible camera with the room's governing axis, stage measured gestures against repeated architecture, preserve formal light and exact color, and let one irregular body disturb order without corridor cliché.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a trainee waits outside a review room.
- Translation: she aligns a folder with the floor seam then stops; a measured wide witness uses repeated doors and ceiling fixtures to hold her small procedural break under flat institutional light.

## 导演：terrence-malick

# Terrence Malick

## Identity
- Chinese name: 泰伦斯·马力克
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Days of Heaven*, *The Thin Red Line*, *The Tree of Life*
- Best suited for: bodily memory, natural light, tactile intervals, environment-led movement.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a sensory interval before or after a decision. Air, cloth, skin, grass, paper, floor texture, and changing light interrupt purposeful action and carry emotion through bodily duration.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use real sun, cloud, dusk, windows, reflected surfaces, and practical darkness; natural light need not be beautiful.
- Shadow and contrast: allow faces and bodies to pass through uneven exposure, backlight, dead interiors, and unstable local contrast.
- Highlights and visibility: sun, projection, water, or cloth may briefly wash or clip a surface while other parts remain dull.
- Reject: perfume-ad backlight, permanent golden hour, polished window glow, or a beautiful spotlight around the subject.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use changing material color rather than a golden wash; mixed practical contamination is allowed.
- Skin and midtones: permit dull, underexposed, or briefly overrun skin as bodies cross light.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate unresolved corners, clipped source portions, breathing midtones, and exposure drift caused by movement.
- Reject: pastoral amber preset, prestige grey-green, global haze, or skin beautification.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor nearby physically possible wide-angle proximity, often with a 24–28mm spatial feeling, partial bodies, and interrupted gestures.
- Height and position: stay near shoulder, hand, waist, or moving body where a human operator could follow.
- Movement and focus: use one-step drift, turn, or short follow motivated by body or air; allow focus breathing and momentary loss of alignment.
- Witness role: a tactile participant, not a diagrammatic observer; reject telephoto crop masquerading as intimacy and polished steadicam display.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let one tactile event outrank procedural clarity.
- Layers and obstruction: use shoulders, sleeves, paper, grass, doorways, curtains, or moving air to interrupt the frame.
- Blocking and geometry: bodies pass through environment rather than align with objects for clean comparison.
- Information control: keep fixed facts present but incomplete; do not make face, hands, props, and room equally legible.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the sensory beat just before or after purposeful action.
- Character action: touch, lower, turn, step, lose alignment, or pause as environment intervenes.
- Visual center: privilege one tactile interruption.
- Blocking: keep the camera bodily close and let the subject cross its light or frame.
- Environment: make air, cloth, paper, vegetation, or temperature alter rhythm.

## Capture Texture
Use varied skin, cloth, paper, and air; breathing focus; captured softness; uneven exposure; and partial bodies. Texture must arise from proximity and movement, never prestige grain or pastoral polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Reducing the method to golden hour.
- Inserting random nature details.
- Directing people to touch plants or stare at the sky.
- Turning close drifting camera into perfume advertising.
- Making every surface lyrical and equally visible.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Chloé Zhao, Malick is more fragmentary and bodily invasive: exposure and focus breathe more aggressively, wide camera proximity interrupts gestures, composition sacrifices completeness to tactile events, while Zhao holds calmer horizon-based natural light, earth-neutral color, respectful medium distance, and community within working landscapes.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use bodily proximity, a physically possible drifting wide camera, tactile interruption, uneven available-light exposure, changing material color, breathing focus, partial bodies, and environment carrying emotion without golden-hour beauty shorthand.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the sensory beat around one tactile interruption; move a nearby wide camera one human step with the body, let focus and exposure briefly lose perfection, keep facts partial, and allow cloth, paper, air, or skin to outrank procedural clarity.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a detective has just compared an employee card with a projected face.
- Translation: the card lowers as a curled paper edge catches his sleeve; a shoulder-close wide witness drifts one step through mixed fluorescent and projector light, breaking alignment and focus while evidence remains peripheral.

## 导演：tsai-ming-liang

# Tsai Ming-liang

## Identity
- Chinese name: 蔡明亮
- Region: Taiwan / Malaysia
- Representative works: *Vive L'Amour*, *The Hole*, *Goodbye, Dragon Inn*
- Best suited for: urban isolation, bodily routine, architectural vacancy, prolonged waiting.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a mundane bodily action that continues beyond comfort. Architecture, leakage, silence, and waiting expose loneliness without explanation.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use fixed room practicals, corridor lights, screen spill, window grey, or water reflections already in the space.
- Shadow and contrast: keep broad dead zones, flat institutional areas, and stubborn pockets of darkness without mood sculpting.
- Highlights and visibility: wet tile, skin, plastic, or a distant screen may hold limited highlights while most of the room remains dull.
- Reject: lyrical haze, portrait keys, dramatic noir cuts, or atmospheric light that romanticizes vacancy.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve sickly paint, concrete, tile, faded upholstery, fluorescent contamination, and isolated object color.
- Skin and midtones: let skin look ordinary, tired, humid, or pallid within the room.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: accept dead midtones, dim corners, small clipped practicals, and long unchanging exposure.
- Reject: tasteful desaturation, dreamy green wash, polished minimalism, or neon melancholy.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use fixed wide or medium-wide observation that holds body and architecture in uncomfortable duration.
- Height and position: place the camera at a room edge, corridor end, auditorium, or doorway where it can remain.
- Movement and focus: lock off for long observation; use deep or middle-deep focus and move rarely.
- Witness role: an unmoving co-occupant, not surveillance and not a drifting poet; reject explanatory coverage and handheld sympathy.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a body occupy only part of a vacant or decaying room.
- Layers and obstruction: use corridors, doorways, screens, leaks, furniture, and empty seating as persistent spatial facts.
- Blocking and geometry: make movements sparse, lateral, repetitive, or delayed; distances remain unresolved.
- Information control: reveal the whole physical predicament while withholding social explanation and emotional release.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose waiting after everyone else has left or routine continuing in isolation.
- Character action: eat, wash, mop, sit, cross, or listen for too long.
- Visual center: share attention between body and vacancy.
- Blocking: keep gestures minimal and distances severe.
- Environment: make leakage, humidity, empty seating, or mechanical sound visible through traces.

## Capture Texture
Use fixed duration, dull surfaces, humid skin, dead fluorescent color, and unembellished spatial sound-like stillness. Avoid attractive minimalism.

## Common Misreadings
- Confusing stillness with an empty frame.
- Adding rain or leaks as symbols only.
- Making loneliness visually pretty.
- Directing actors to appear catatonic.
- Using deep focus without bodily duration.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Hou Hsiao-hsien, Tsai strips away layered social circulation: flatter dead practical light, isolated sickly color, fixed wide camera, and severe vacancy around repetitive bodies rather than inhabited source light, muted material color, medium-long multi-plane observation, and several everyday actions sharing duration.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a fixed wide witness, dead source-based light, isolated worn color, severe architectural vacancy, deep or middle-deep focus, minimal repetitive action, and duration that makes bodily routine uncomfortable.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as an ordinary action continuing too long after others leave; lock a camera at the room edge, keep body and vacancy equally present, accept dull practical exposure and dead color, and withhold relief or explanatory close-ups.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: the last projectionist cleans a cinema after closure.
- Translation: he mops beneath a still-lit exit sign while rainwater enters the empty aisle; a locked auditorium view holds his small repeated path, dead red seats, green wall paint, and distant screen spill.
