# Default Premium Quality Router

Use this module whenever the user asks for a premium/high-end TVC but gives no approved visual style, or
when a supplied direction consists only of labels such as “cinematic”, “luxury” or “international”. It is
a decision router, not a house LUT and not permission to imitate any source film.

## 1. Shared default quality floor

Apply all of the following before selecting a category branch:

1. **One dominant visual engine.** State the recurring relationship that generates the film's images:
   reveal by traveling light, motif across scale, silhouette proximity, directional performance, or another
   brief-specific rule. Decorative devices must support it.
2. **Restricted color roles.** Assign a base world, subject/product separation family, one focal/brand accent
   and at most one temporary event color. Do not inherit black/gold, red or any source palette by default.
3. **Authored tonal architecture.** Declare what remains visible, what may fall into silhouette, the black
   floor, highlight roll-off or designed clipping, and the cue that preserves hierarchy in grayscale.
4. **Motivated reveal light.** State source geometry, path, affected surfaces and stop. Product highlights
   reveal construction; human light reveals gesture, state or relation.
5. **Material differentiation.** Give every hero material its own roughness, specular width, edge/contact and
   motion response. Never let skin, paint, metal, glass and fabric collapse into one AI gloss.
6. **Graphic hierarchy and negative space.** Define the first attention target, the supporting layer, the
   readable product/face/logo window and the protected area for Super when applicable.
7. **Scale progression.** Move between detail, subject/product and hero/icon only when each scale adds evidence.
8. **One camera intention per shot.** Describe motivation, start relation, path, speed curve and landing frame.
   Complexity may come from blocking, light, edit or state change rather than extra camera axes.
9. **Temporal contrast.** Alternate readable holds with event bursts. A fast reference does not authorize
   constant speed; a restrained reference does not require slow motion everywhere.
10. **Motif and resolution.** Repeat a bounded motif in altered states, then land on a stable hero, proof or
    brand frame. Protect product topology, CMF, identity and legal information throughout.

If these decisions cannot be inferred safely, use the neutral fallback: restrained authored product language,
controlled tonal hierarchy, limited palette with one focal accent, motivated reveal light, differentiated
materials, deliberate negative space, one camera intention, temporal contrast and a stable hero landing.

## 2. Branch router

Choose the branch from product truth, dramatic promise and audience—not from whichever reference looks most
spectacular. A film may use one primary branch and one bounded secondary device; do not average all branches.

| Branch | Route when | Primary evidence | Default motion logic | Do not inherit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automotive product | Exterior/interior form, lamp signature, stance, motion or high-value vehicle craft carries the promise | Body volume, aerodynamic continuity, lamp/brand identity, paint/glass response, full-vehicle hero | Low, mass-aware paths; traveling reflections; macro-to-profile reveal; stable proof hold | Phone-scale rotations, generic gold edges, constant orbit |
| Smartphone concept/product | Industrial design, interface restraint, component construction or hand-scale precision carries the promise | Edge/chamfer/port/button/camera-module geometry, glass-metal separation, tactile scale, feature window | Controlled rotation or controlled light, rarely both at high speed; inspection holds | Vehicle stance logic, body-roll language, oversized sweeping reflections |
| Metaphoric material/scale | Innovation or transformation needs an idea larger than literal demonstration | One motif repeated across cosmic, human, product and material scales | Tableaux, gesture/light anchors, bounded exposure or state events, motif return | Source celestial objects, hand pose, palette or title design |
| Chromatic silhouette intimacy | Sensory relation, desire, closeness or emotional distance is the product territory | Body separation, profiles/hands, near-touch, edge readability, one chromatic world with tonal depth | Precise blocking; locked or simple camera; brief intensity events | Mandatory red, explicit poses, uniform bloom, face loss |
| Graphic kinetic performance | Sport, fashion, youth energy or cultural velocity drives persuasion | Directional action, recurring icon/product motifs, graphic scale changes, anchor holds | Short event bursts around protected identity/product frames; distortion is phase-bounded | Constant strobe, arbitrary inversion, identity-destroying smear |

## 3. Product-branch separation

Automotive and smartphone work are siblings under sculptural product authorship, not interchangeable templates.

- **Automotive camera/light vocabulary:** front/rear signature, shoulder and roof continuity, long curved panels,
  wheel/road axis, stance, mass, moving environmental reflections and a readable full-vehicle profile.
- **Smartphone camera/light vocabulary:** edge radius, chamfer, seam, port, button, lens stack, display/glass plane,
  coating, hand scale, assembly precision and feature-by-feature inspection.
- Shared controls are limited to protected geometry, restrained staging, motivated reveal light, negative space
  and confident landing frames.

## 4. Prompt construction

Write the global block in this order:

`brief/product cause → selected branch and dominant visual engine → tonal architecture → color roles → motivated
light geometry → category-specific materials/evidence → composition/negative space → optical behaviour → temporal
contrast/motif → hero landing → prohibited inheritance → acceptance checks`

Write each motion block in this order:

`start state → category evidence to reveal → subject/product change → camera motivation and path → light/reflection
response → readable hold → deceleration/landing → edit handoff → approved VO/SFX/silence anchor → invariants`

Do not put source titles, brands, directors, exact shots, source colors, choreography or edit order into the
model-facing prompt. Follow `audio-policy.md`: visual prompts contain no music direction.

## 5. Acceptance gates

- The film has one named visual engine and one primary branch.
- The branch can be justified from the brief without naming a reference.
- Removing “premium/cinematic/luxury” leaves a complete visual instruction.
- Product geometry and feature evidence remain readable at the landing frame.
- Camera, light and material changes share one causal event rather than competing.
- At least one intentional hold exists for every major claim/product state.
- Fast flashes, inversion, silhouette or deep black have bounded duration and a readability recovery.
- Automotive and smartphone vocabulary have not been substituted for each other.
- The result differs from the references in brand assets, palette, motif, choreography and exact sequencing.
