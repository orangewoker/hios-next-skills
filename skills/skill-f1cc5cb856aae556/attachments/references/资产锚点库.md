# 资产锚点库（成片四资产）

成片 Skill 的 4 类复用资产：角色锚点 / 场景锚点 / 道具锚点 / 分镜锚点。复制即用。

---

## 一、角色锚点（按亚型）

| 亚型 | 面部/神情 | 头发 | 翼/光环 | 装饰 |
|------|-----------|------|---------|------|
| 天使（线稿） | `razored pointed chin, vacant gaze past viewer, androgynous` | `long flowing hair, few decisive lines, ends dissolve` | `wings as few radiating long lines, thin gold circle halo` | 极简 |
| 古典女性（彩稿） | `porcelain pale skin, eyes half-closed, divine detachment` | `elaborate headdress hair, long lines, gold ornaments` | 多无翼 | `crown, forehead jewel, earrings, layered robes` |
| 妖精（线稿） | `androgynous, flower crown, vacant pupils` | `petal-like, few lines` | `tiny line-wings / crescent moon instead of halo` | `flower crown` |
| 神话/妖异（彩稿） | `androgynous, sorrowful void` | `long dark hair, few lines` | `broken/dim wings` | `horns, non-human marks` |

**头发统一锚点（用户纠正关键，全亚型必含）**：
```
long flowing hair rendered in a few decisive sweeping ink lines,
hair sharing the same floating breath as the wings,
ends dissolving into white, never a solid mass
```

---

## 二、场景锚点

| 模式 | 背景 | 留白/底 | 点缀元素 |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| 线稿 | 白/极淡桑纸 | 留白≥65%，有极淡银丝气线 | `crescent moon, drifting silver motes, faint petals` |
| 彩稿 | 黑/深靛/深褐 | 金箔微微发光 | `gold particles, ornate patterns, floating embers` |

**场景气连锚点**：`vast empty space alive with faint motes, so the emptiness breathes with the figure`

---

## 三、道具锚点

| 道具 | 线稿表达 | 彩稿表达 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 翼 | `wings suggested by overlapping fine lines, few decisive strokes` | 同左，可叠金线 |
| 光环 | `thin gold line circle, not glowing mass` | `thin gold line halo, delicate` |
| 衣袂 | `sash/robe as few sweeping lines, floating` | `layered ornate robes, gold thread` |
| 装饰 | 极简 | `jewels, forehead ornament, crescent crown` |

---

## 四、分镜锚点（8 列模板见 分镜与prompt模板.md）

每个成片建议 5–8 镜，单镜 3–10s，情绪曲线贯穿。视觉主符号（翼/光环/发）重复≥2 次且首尾呼应。
