# Case library

These seed cases are synthetic reusable patterns, not copies of existing campaigns. Replace or extend them with evidence-backed cases using `knowledge-update.md`.

## SEED-01 — Dark wireless earbuds, 15-second launch

- Date/model/tool: seed pattern; untested across a specific model
- Input conditions: black earbuds and charging case, 9:16, 15 seconds, one product reference
- Goal: make a small product feel precise and architectural without losing recognition
- Pattern combination: O05 axial tunnel → F03 mesh texture → F06 hinge → F05 magnetic settle → E06 functional ending
- Identity lock: bud stem length, mesh count impression, case hinge, left/right bud orientation
- Prompt move that worked conceptually: begin at a recognizable circular acoustic opening, then exit macro before the view becomes abstract for too long
- Observed result: design hypothesis only
- Failure/repair: black-on-black can swallow the case; use two cool edge strips and a restrained warm interior pulse
- Reuse boundary: useful for products with circular openings; avoid invented internal drivers
- Confidence: low
