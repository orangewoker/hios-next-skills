# 运镜技法

> **定位**：运镜 Prompt 库。Phase 2 D5.4 运镜策略选型时查询。每个运镜写明方向+速度，禁止只写"动态镜头"。

## 基础运镜

| 技法 | 英文关键词 | 叙事功能 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **推镜** (Dolly In) | `slow dolly in, camera pushing forward toward subject` | 逼近/聚焦/情绪升温 |
| **拉镜** (Dolly Out) | `slow dolly out, camera pulling back from subject` | 疏离/揭示环境/孤独 |
| **摇镜** (Pan) | `smooth pan left/right, camera rotating on tripod` | 扫描空间/跟随视线 |
| **俯仰** (Tilt) | `slow tilt up/down, camera tilting vertically` | 揭示高度/权力展示 |
| **跟镜** (Tracking) | `tracking shot following subject, side parallel movement` | 陪伴/跟随/旅程感 |
| **升镜** (Crane Up) | `crane shot rising, camera elevating smoothly` | 超越/升华/俯瞰全局 |
| **降镜** (Crane Down) | `crane shot descending, camera lowering` | 降临/落地/进入场景 |
| **手持** (Handheld) | `slight handheld camera, subtle natural shake` | 纪实/紧张/真实感 |
| **稳定器** (Steadicam) | `steadicam shot, smooth floating movement` | 流畅跟随/沉浸感 |
| **轨道环绕** (Orbit) | `orbital camera movement, circling around subject` | 审视/全方位展示 |

## 特殊运镜

| 技法 | 英文关键词 | 叙事功能 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **快速变焦** (Crash Zoom) | `crash zoom, rapid zoom in on subject` | 冲击/震惊/喜剧 |
| **甩镜** (Whip Pan) | `whip pan, fast blurred camera swing` | 能量转换/场景切换 |
| **变焦推拉** (Dolly Zoom) | `dolly zoom, vertigo effect, background compresses while subject stays` | 心理崩塌/认知颠覆 |
| **焦点转移** (Rack Focus) | `rack focus, focus shifts from foreground to background` | 揭示/注意力转移 |
| **慢动作** (Slow Motion) | `slow motion, time decelerates to emphasize moment` | 情绪高潮/关键瞬间 |
| **延时** (Time-lapse) | `time-lapse, accelerated passage of time` | 时间流逝/场景过渡 |

## 运镜转写规则

每个运动镜头必须写明：起点方向 → 速度（缓/常/急）→ 停点。
```
✓ 「摄影机从门口方向缓推进至收银台，停在她的背影后」
✗ 「运镜」「动态镜头」「高级运镜」
```
