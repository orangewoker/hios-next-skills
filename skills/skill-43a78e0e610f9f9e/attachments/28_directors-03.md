## 导演：bong-joon-ho

# Bong Joon-ho

## Identity
- Chinese name: 奉俊昊
- Region: South Korea
- Representative works: *Memories of Murder*, *Parasite*, *Mother*
- Best suited for: class systems, procedural failure, dark comedy, sudden tonal violence.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a practical task whose social hierarchy becomes visible through space. Comedy, procedure, embarrassment, and danger can coexist until one blocking change reverses control.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, basement windows, fluorescents, domestic fixtures, rain, vehicle lights, and institutional practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: make class or access differences visible through bright and dark levels, thresholds, and weather.
- Highlights and visibility: keep crucial props and reactions readable before a tonal reversal sends part of the frame into harder contrast.
- Reject: moody darkness without social geography, elegant thriller keys, decorative rain, or comedic flatness everywhere.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use believable domestic, institutional, street, and weather color with strategic contrasts between social zones.
- Skin and midtones: keep faces readable enough for group reaction while allowing harsh source shifts during reversal.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold accessible midtones, then use deeper blacks or clipped weather highlights at the turn.
- Reject: uniform green class coding, teal-orange thriller grade, luxury color polish, or cartoon saturation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor wide or normal group views that reveal levels, routes, and reaction chains; use closer frames at reversal.
- Height and position: place the camera on stairs, corridors, rooms, streets, or floor levels where social access is legible.
- Movement and focus: track or reframe precisely with blocking; maintain deep enough focus for setup and background consequence.
- Witness role: a socially alert storyteller, not a neutral institution or flamboyant stylist; reject incoherent handheld chaos.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: connect a practical task to a class boundary, hidden route, or reaction chain.
- Layers and obstruction: use stairs, windows, doors, tables, basements, roads, and weather as social structure.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange ensembles so status changes can be seen through height, access, and movement.
- Information control: reveal more to viewers than to at least one character, enabling dread or dark comedy.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a routine task one beat before social control reverses.
- Character action: carry, serve, search, hide, clean, or wait while another group enters.
- Visual center: privilege the route or boundary where status changes.
- Blocking: keep a chain of reactions across levels.
- Environment: make stairs, rain, doors, or service spaces redistribute power.

## Capture Texture
Use clear spatial capture, ordinary surfaces, weather, bodily awkwardness, and tonal flexibility. Avoid prestige gloss and caricature.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating class themes as visual symbolism only.
- Copying stairs without power logic.
- Making dark comedy cartoonish.
- Using a green filter for poverty.
- Hiding ensemble reactions in shallow focus.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Park Chan-wook, Bong prioritizes social geography and reaction chains: more functional source light, ordinary contrasting color, wide ensemble camera, and status shifts across levels rather than sensual high-contrast lighting, controlled jewel tones, expressive camera geometry, and psychologically charged object-body compositions.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use readable social levels, practical source contrast, ordinary but strategically separated color, wide ensemble geography, precise reaction chains, and a mundane task that tips into danger or dark comedy through blocking.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene one beat before social control reverses; map the relevant levels, doorways, or stairs in a wide group frame, keep setup props and reactions readable, and let one entrance or movement reorder status without losing physical realism.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: catering staff clear a corporate banquet after executives leave.
- Translation: a service elevator opens on an executive returning for a missing phone; a wide kitchen-door view keeps wet floor, stacked dishes, staff reactions, and the raised dining room visible across three levels of practical light.

## 导演：brian-de-palma

# Brian De Palma

## Identity
- Chinese name: 布莱恩·德·帕尔玛
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Carrie*, *Blow Out*, *Carlito's Way*
- Best suited for: surveillance, divided attention, operatic suspense, guilt and spectacle.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a situation where two dangerous actions unfold at once. Make looking itself unstable through divided screens, foreground traps, extended camera paths, or conflicting points of view.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use stage, street, apartment, public-building, practical night, and event lighting with heightened but motivated direction.
- Shadow and contrast: permit sharper theatrical contrast and color separation when surveillance or performance spaces justify it.
- Highlights and visibility: reflective floors, glass, instruments, lenses, and faces may receive graphic highlights that connect watcher and event.
- Reject: unmotivated nightclub glow, generic noir, random spotlights, or darkness that erases simultaneous action.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: allow bolder reds, blues, ambers, skin, costume, and practical-event color organized around danger and performance.
- Skin and midtones: keep faces readable across divided zones even when source colors differ.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: use rich blacks, bright practicals, and controlled saturation without comic-book clipping.
- Reject: arbitrary neon, teal-orange action grade, vintage wash, or color disconnected from spectacle and guilt.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: combine wide spatial setup, long-lens surveillance, split focus, or split-screen-like simultaneous depth when physically coherent.
- Height and position: use balconies, stairs, corridors, streets, stages, windows, or recording positions that expose multiple routes.
- Movement and focus: employ extended tracks, circular moves, crane-like reveals, or exact focus division only when they synchronize threats.
- Witness role: a flamboyant but spatially accountable voyeur, not a generic action camera; reject technique without simultaneous information.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: divide attention between watcher and event, rescue and threat, or foreground clue and background action.
- Layers and obstruction: use rails, glass, stages, doorways, crowds, screens, and recording devices.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph converging routes and delayed recognition across multiple planes.
- Information control: show viewers two pieces of danger that characters cannot combine in time.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose simultaneous actions approaching collision.
- Character action: watch, record, cross, signal, pursue, or miss.
- Visual center: split attention between at least two causal planes.
- Blocking: synchronize routes with a visible deadline.
- Environment: make stairs, stage, crowd, or recording position structure the collision.

## Capture Texture
Use controlled optical sharpness, practical flare, split focus, and physically legible camera movement. Avoid empty technical showmanship and synthetic spectacle.

## Common Misreadings
- Using split screen without simultaneous causality.
- Copying Hitchcock shots.
- Adding saturated color without performance context.
- Making long camera moves spatially impossible.
- Turning suspense into generic action coverage.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Alfred Hitchcock, De Palma displays divided attention more overtly: higher-contrast event light, bolder practical color, elaborate roaming or split-focus camera, and simultaneous planes competing in one design rather than economical source light, disciplined color, concise point-of-view shifts, and a single knowledge mismatch.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use motivated theatrical contrast, bolder practical color, spatially accountable extended camera movement, divided focus or simultaneous planes, and two causal actions converging while different characters hold incomplete knowledge.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as two visible actions nearing collision; position the camera where both routes can coexist, use one controlled extended move or split-depth relation to transfer attention, and heighten practical light without losing geography.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a sound technician sees a cyclist approach a blocked tunnel while recording from a bridge.
- Translation: a long lateral move keeps the recorder and waveform foregrounded as the cyclist and an unmarked car converge below under sodium lamps and red maintenance lights.

## 导演：chloe-zhao

# Chloé Zhao

## Identity
- Chinese name: 赵婷
- Region: China / United States
- Representative works: *Songs My Brothers Taught Me*, *The Rider*, *Nomadland*
- Best suited for: working landscapes, nonprofessional presence, community, quiet resilience.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a practical action within a real working landscape or community. Let dignity emerge from routine, horizon, weather, and shared labor without turning hardship into uplift.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use open sky, dawn, dusk, windows, camp or work lights, and reflected ground light.
- Shadow and contrast: favor broad natural contrast with faces moving between sky fill and practical shade; night remains genuinely limited.
- Highlights and visibility: dust, metal, skin, grass, vehicles, and fire catch restrained natural highlights.
- Reject: permanent golden-hour backlight, inspirational rim light, perfume-like flare, or polished wilderness illumination.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep earth, sky, work clothes, vehicles, trailers, interiors, and regional weather natural and subdued.
- Skin and midtones: retain sun, wind, age, and varied skin without cosmetic warming.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: protect sky, allow open shadows and practical night falloff, and keep midtones gentle but not pastel.
- Reject: amber resilience grade, cyan landscape wash, faded Americana, or sentimental desaturation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use respectful medium or medium-wide normal-to-wide observation that keeps body and working environment connected.
- Height and position: stand or sit with the community at human eye level, near a vehicle, doorway, fence, camp, or field.
- Movement and focus: follow modestly or remain still; use contextual depth and avoid intrusive focus tricks.
- Witness role: a respectful participant with personal space, not a lyrical body-fragment collector; reject aggressive proximity and sweeping drone spectacle.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a practical gesture and horizon or community share the frame.
- Layers and obstruction: use vehicles, fences, tools, doorways, weather, and other workers as lived context.
- Blocking and geometry: keep people naturally spaced by work and landscape rather than symbolically isolated.
- Information control: show labor and environment clearly while withholding easy triumph or tragedy.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose work or travel continuing after a private setback.
- Character action: repair, feed, pack, drive, tend, or share an object.
- Visual center: balance body, task, and landscape.
- Blocking: include community at respectful distance when present.
- Environment: make weather and working conditions influence pace.

## Capture Texture
Use natural skin, dust, wind, worn tools, open-sky exposure, and quiet handheld or stable presence. Avoid pastoral polish and poverty tourism.

## Common Misreadings
- Using golden hour for every scene.
- Making subjects noble silhouettes.
- Treating landscape as inspirational wallpaper.
- Romanticizing poverty or itinerancy.
- Copying Malick-like body fragments and flare.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Terrence Malick, Zhao preserves respectful completeness and social context: calmer open-sky light, earth-neutral color, medium-distance camera, and bodies situated in work and community rather than unstable exposure, fragmentary wide proximity, breathing focus, and tactile interruptions outranking task clarity.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use broad natural source light, earth-neutral material color, respectful medium-wide observation, contextual focus, practical labor, community spacing, and landscape influencing routine without inspirational beauty.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect a work gesture continuing after setback; observe from a calm human distance, keep task, person, and working landscape in one relation, protect sky and natural skin, and refuse golden-hour uplift or fragmentary lyrical intrusion.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a seasonal worker packs a van after losing the next job.
- Translation: she repairs a torn glove before closing the rear doors while another worker coils a hose in the distance; a medium-wide eye-level view holds cold dawn sky, dust, tools, and quiet community.
