## 导演：martin-scorsese

# Martin Scorsese

## Identity
- Chinese name: 马丁·斯科塞斯
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Taxi Driver*, *Goodfellas*, *Raging Bull*
- Best suited for: volatile identity, street institutions, temptation, guilt, social performance.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant a character performs confidence while appetite, shame, or danger leaks through behavior. Social spaces judge and energize the person at once.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use bars, street lamps, headlights, restaurants, dressing rooms, apartments, arenas, and church or club practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: allow nervous pools, hard source transitions, reflected street light, and localized darkness around social pressure.
- Highlights and visibility: sweat, mirrors, chrome, smoke from real rooms, glass, and eyes may flash within restless environments.
- Reject: generic gangster low key, glossy neon, constant red light, or shadow detached from social place.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use volatile source color—street red, bar amber, fluorescent green, flash white, restaurant warmth—without a uniform grade.
- Skin and midtones: let skin shift with appetite, heat, fatigue, and social setting.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hot practicals, firm blacks, and changing midtones while reactions stay readable.
- Reject: teal-orange crime grade, nostalgic Italian warmth, desaturated grit, or saturated color without behavioral pressure.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use assertive wide or normal proximity, subjective longer-lens observation, and closer frames when performance cracks.
- Height and position: move through street, club, apartment, car, kitchen, or arena at participant height.
- Movement and focus: track, pan, whip, or hold with behavioral motivation; use focus and speed to shift complicity.
- Witness role: an implicated social participant, not a solemn institutional observer; reject empty bravura and random handheld frenzy.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place the performed self against judging faces, mirrors, or a social route.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, bars, cars, mirrors, crowds, doors, smoke, and street traffic.
- Blocking and geometry: let status appear through entrances, seating, who follows whom, and who commands movement.
- Information control: reveal seductive access before exposing cost or isolation.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant confidence overreaches or private guilt interrupts performance.
- Character action: enter, boast, watch, rehearse, follow, lash out, or withdraw.
- Visual center: privilege the crack between self-image and social reality.
- Blocking: keep witnesses and status routes active.
- Environment: make club, street, workplace, or family space intensify behavior.

## Capture Texture
Use restless practical exposure, sweat, smoke, glass, street reflections, and motivated camera energy. Avoid gangster glamour and applied grit.

## Common Misreadings
- Reducing the style to gangsters.
- Using long tracking shots without social access.
- Making every scene red and smoky.
- Treating violence as cool spectacle.
- Applying handheld grit instead of behavior.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Francis Ford Coppola, Scorsese is more volatile and participant-driven: sharper shifting practical light, restless social color, mobile wide-or-normal camera, and status enacted through entrances and performance rather than dense hierarchical shadow, restrained warmth, measured observation, and inherited power organized across ritual depth.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use volatile practical light, shifting social color, implicated camera proximity, entrances and mirrors that expose status, readable witnesses, and a performed identity cracking under appetite, shame, or danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a character's social performance overreaches; move or hold the camera from a participant position that reveals who watches, let practical color and exposure shift with the room, and make the crack in self-image control the frame.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a junior broker enters a late-night diner after losing client money.
- Translation: he rehearses a confident phone call while the cook and two colleagues watch through the mirror; a short counter track uses fluorescent green, red street spill, and chrome reflections to expose the performance.

## 导演：michael-mann

# Michael Mann

## Identity
- Chinese name: 迈克尔·曼
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Heat*, *The Insider*, *Collateral*
- Best suited for: professional systems, nocturnal cities, surveillance, procedure under personal cost.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a professional action executed with precision while personal consequence enters at the edge. City infrastructure and technology become real working environments, not futuristic decoration.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use sodium streets, mercury or LED spill, office fluorescents, vehicle dashboards, airport or industrial fixtures, and early digital low-light response.
- Shadow and contrast: build broad city darkness punctuated by real infrastructure sources; faces may be uneven but tasks remain legible.
- Highlights and visibility: glass towers, wet roads, metal, screens, headlights, and skin catch hard urban highlights.
- Reject: cyberpunk neon, glossy blue rim light, universal darkness, or luxury-car commercial lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use mixed urban cyan, sodium amber, green fluorescent, concrete, steel, and night sky according to actual sources.
- Skin and midtones: allow source contamination and digital low-light coarseness without plastic correction.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate noisy or thin blacks, clipped practicals, open night midtones, and source-driven white-balance conflict.
- Reject: teal-orange action grade, magenta cyberpunk, polished night blue, or smooth cinematic black.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: combine precise medium professional observation, long-lens surveillance, and mobile wide or normal proximity during operations.
- Height and position: occupy vehicles, rooftops, offices, streets, terminals, or tactical routes at actual working positions.
- Movement and focus: track efficiently with procedure, use long-lens compression across city space, and retain enough focus for infrastructure.
- Witness role: a skilled professional aware of systems and risk, not a glamorous action spectator; reject impossible chase cameras.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: connect a professional gesture to a network, route, city grid, or approaching personal consequence.
- Layers and obstruction: use glass, traffic, vehicles, monitors, fences, roads, and architectural grids.
- Blocking and geometry: stage teams and solitary figures through operational lines, cross-city separation, and timed movement.
- Information control: make the task clear while keeping emotional cost at the edge until it intrudes.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose competent execution just as personal risk becomes visible.
- Character action: monitor, drive, load, wait, call, align, or abort.
- Visual center: bind person to tool and urban system.
- Blocking: keep professional routes exact and separated.
- Environment: let traffic, towers, terminals, or industrial networks constrain timing.

## Capture Texture
Use real nocturnal source contamination, glass, steel, road reflections, digital low-light grain or natural film response, and practical operational detail. Avoid luxury advertising.

## Common Misreadings
- Calling all blue night scenes Michael Mann-like.
- Turning cities into cyberpunk.
- Glamorizing cars and weapons.
- Hiding procedure in shallow focus.
- Smoothing digital low-light texture.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Fincher, Mann expands procedure into live urban networks: harsher mixed infrastructure light, more open source-conflicted color, mobile and long-lens city observation, and operational routes across distance rather than controlled low illumination, restrained neutral color, exact room-bound camera, and evidence chains organized on work surfaces.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use real mixed city sources, source-conflicted night color, professional camera positions, mobile or long-lens operational observation, infrastructure depth, and precise work disrupted by personal consequence.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect competent action as personal risk enters the frame; place the camera on the actual operational route, keep tool, city network, and timing legible, accept mixed-source night exposure and coarse blacks, and refuse cyberpunk or luxury polish.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an airport technician discovers a familiar name on a cargo manifest.
- Translation: he keeps scanning containers while calling from the tug lane; a long-lens terminal view compresses sodium ramps, cyan hangar light, moving loaders, and his still figure against the operational flow.

## 导演：park-chan-wook

# Park Chan-wook

## Identity
- Chinese name: 朴赞郁
- Region: South Korea
- Representative works: *Oldboy*, *The Handmaiden*, *Decision to Leave*
- Best suited for: desire, revenge, deception, psychologically charged objects and spaces.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an action in which desire, control, and concealment share one object or spatial relation. Visual elegance should sharpen moral discomfort, not replace it.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, lamps, candles, screens, exterior weather, or institutional practicals with carefully controlled direction.
- Shadow and contrast: build sculpted local contrast, reflected light, and sharp transitions that bind faces to objects or architecture.
- Highlights and visibility: skin, fabric, metal, glass, wood, or water may receive sensual highlights while motive remains shadowed.
- Reject: random colored glamour, glossy rim light, universal low-key mood, or beauty illumination without psychological function.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use controlled jewel, earth, green, blue, red, or neutral relations tied to costume, décor, landscape, and source light.
- Skin and midtones: let skin respond precisely to nearby material and reflected color.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: maintain rich blacks, protected whites, and shaped midtones with selective saturation.
- Reject: arbitrary teal-orange, neon revenge grading, oversaturated luxury, or color motifs disconnected from desire and control.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: alternate precise close object-body relations with wider architectural reveals; lens choice may create elegant unease.
- Height and position: use drawers, mirrors, stairs, ceilings, corridors, tables, or landscape lines as physically motivated viewpoints.
- Movement and focus: employ controlled tracks, pivots, overheads, or focus transfers when they reveal concealed relation.
- Witness role: an exact conspiratorial observer, not a chaotic victim; reject movement that exists only for virtuosity.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: bind a charged object to a hand, face, or hidden observer.
- Layers and obstruction: use mirrors, screens, doors, fabric, furniture, stairs, and landscape folds to double or conceal relationships.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph bodies through precise lines, reversals, and visual rhymes.
- Information control: reveal one layer elegantly while withholding the power relation behind it.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant an intimate gesture becomes evidence or control.
- Character action: fold, exchange, observe, hide, fasten, wash, or touch an object.
- Visual center: connect the object to two competing desires.
- Blocking: create a precise triangle among actor, object, and observer.
- Environment: make décor, weather, or architecture participate in concealment.

## Capture Texture
Use tactile fabric, wood, skin, metal, water, and controlled optical transitions. Preserve material sensuality without fashion-ad polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating elegance as the whole style.
- Using violent imagery without desire or control.
- Applying jewel tones indiscriminately.
- Adding overhead shots without a concealment function.
- Turning sensual surfaces into luxury advertising.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Bong Joon-ho, Park compresses social meaning into charged object-body geometry: more sculpted light, controlled saturated palettes, expressive precise camera moves, and triangular concealment rather than functional source light, ordinary social color, wide ensemble geography, and status reversals across levels.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use sculpted motivated light, controlled material color, precise object-body relations, expressive but physical camera geometry, mirrored or layered concealment, and an intimate gesture that becomes evidence of desire or control.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a beautiful or ordinary gesture changes power; construct a precise triangle among body, object, and observer, shape source light and material color around that relation, and use one controlled camera reveal without luxury polish.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a translator returns a borrowed fountain pen after a secret meeting.
- Translation: her thumb wipes ink from the clip while the owner watches through a mirror; a slow table-height pivot joins hand, reflected eye, and locked drawer under window rain and one green-shaded lamp.
