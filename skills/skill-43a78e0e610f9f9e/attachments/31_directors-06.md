## 导演：francis-ford-coppola

# Francis Ford Coppola

## Identity
- Chinese name: 弗朗西斯·福特·科波拉
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *The Godfather*, *The Conversation*, *Apocalypse Now*
- Best suited for: family power, secrecy, moral compromise, institutions becoming mythic.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a private decision whose consequences extend through family, organization, or state power. Silence, listening, ritual, and withheld reaction make authority feel inherited and corrosive.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, shaded lamps, practical interiors, exterior heat, fire, or public-event light with strong motivated direction.
- Shadow and contrast: allow faces to emerge from dense rooms, broad top shadow, or layered practical pools while power centers remain legible.
- Highlights and visibility: polished wood, smoke from real activity, uniforms, sweat, glass, and eyes catch restrained highlights.
- Reject: generic gangster darkness, universal top light, glossy sepia, or theatrical shadow without power logic.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use warm interiors, dark wood, skin, olive, black clothing, exterior heat, or surveillance neutrals according to setting.
- Skin and midtones: let skin become warm, sallow, or dim through actual room sources and moral atmosphere.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold deep blacks, protected lamps and windows, and rich midtones without crushing every face.
- Reject: blanket amber Godfather grade, teal-orange war color, vintage brown wash, or luxury polish.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor measured medium or longer-lens observation for power and listening; use wides when institutional or landscape scale expands consequence.
- Height and position: occupy table edges, doorways, listening posts, crowds, boats, or rooms where alliances can be read.
- Movement and focus: use patient pushes, pans, or restrained tracks when moral alignment changes; preserve layered reactions.
- Witness role: a grave participant listening for consequence, not a frenetic street observer; reject showy movement.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: link a quiet decision to a family or institutional hierarchy.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, tables, curtains, crowds, recording equipment, and depth to conceal allegiance.
- Blocking and geometry: place authority centrally or deeply while petitioners and witnesses occupy subordinate planes.
- Information control: reveal who controls the room while withholding the private cost of that control.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose assent, refusal, or listening before irreversible consequence.
- Character action: receive, close a door, lower a voice, listen, sign, or remain seated.
- Visual center: privilege the transfer of authority.
- Blocking: use layered hierarchy and withheld reaction.
- Environment: let family ritual, office protocol, or public event carry institutional weight.

## Capture Texture
Use rich but source-bound darkness, tactile wood, fabric, heat, smoke, and patient focus. Avoid gangster pastiche and prestige sepia.

## Common Misreadings
- Applying amber darkness to every interior.
- Treating organized crime as the only subject.
- Using solemnity without moral consequence.
- Copying famous compositions.
- Crushing faces into unreadable black.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Martin Scorsese, Coppola is slower and more hierarchical: denser source-bound shadow, richer restrained warmth, measured medium or long observation, and layered ritual power rather than nervous practical contrast, volatile street color, mobile participant camera, and behavior erupting through social performance.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use dense motivated interior shadow, restrained rich material color, measured observation, layered family or institutional hierarchy, patient reaction, and a quiet decision whose consequences extend beyond the room.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat when authority silently changes hands; place a measured camera where petition, witness, and power occupy distinct depths, let practical pools and dense shadow conceal cost, and keep the gesture quieter than its consequence.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a family business decides whether to dismiss a longtime driver.
- Translation: the eldest sibling folds the driver's keys into a cloth while the father remains behind a half-closed study door; a measured dining-room view uses shaded lamps and window dusk to layer petition, decision, and inherited authority.

## 导演：hirokazu-koreeda

# Hirokazu Kore-eda

## Identity
- Chinese name: 是枝裕和
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Still Walking*, *Shoplifters*, *After the Storm*
- Best suited for: family behavior, moral ambiguity, children, ordinary household time.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an overlapping household action in which care, resentment, and memory coexist. Let ethical complexity emerge through who notices, serves, waits, or avoids rather than through formal declaration.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, kitchen fixtures, ceiling lights, television spill, and weather-soft daylight.
- Shadow and contrast: keep flexible gentle contrast that follows people between rooms; allow lived darker corners without formal emphasis.
- Highlights and visibility: food, dishes, laundry, skin, and rain receive modest everyday highlights.
- Reject: sentimental glow, portrait isolation, formal noir shadow, or perfect domestic exposure.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve warm-neutral wood, food, clothes, plastic, walls, and seasonal daylight without nostalgia.
- Skin and midtones: keep varied natural skin within shared household light.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: use broad midtones, protected windows, soft blacks, and small practical inconsistencies.
- Reject: sepia family memory, pastel coziness, cool social-drama wash, or beautified poverty.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use normal-lens medium or medium-wide observation that can move between individual gesture and group behavior.
- Height and position: remain at ordinary standing or seated eye level near a doorway, table, kitchen, or hallway.
- Movement and focus: hold patiently, reframe gently, or follow briefly; keep enough depth for overlapping behavior.
- Witness role: a trusted but ethically alert family observer, not an invisible formal authority; reject showy handheld intimacy and rigid low-camera imitation.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow a small care gesture and an avoided relationship to compete.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, doorways, kitchen counters, laundry, and household traffic naturally.
- Blocking and geometry: let family members overlap, interrupt, serve, and leave according to routine.
- Information control: reveal behavior generously while withholding definitive judgment.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose routine before or after a difficult family statement.
- Character action: serve food, fold clothes, listen from another room, or clean while someone waits.
- Visual center: share emphasis between action and unspoken relationship.
- Blocking: overlap family routes rather than isolate each person.
- Environment: let household work continue and carry history.

## Capture Texture
Use natural skin, food, fabric, rain, and ordinary interior wear with flexible exposure and unobtrusive focus. Avoid cozy commercial polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every family scene warm and comforting.
- Copying Ozu's low locked camera.
- Treating children as symbols of innocence.
- Beautifying cramped homes.
- Explaining moral meaning through close-ups.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Yasujiro Ozu, Kore-eda is more mobile, eye-level, and behaviorally overlapping: flexible household light, warm-neutral color, gentle reframing, and family routes crossing the frame rather than low locked planes, quiet exact material color, formal separation, and empty space carrying aftermath.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use eye-level normal-lens household observation, flexible window and practical light, warm-neutral material color, overlapping family behavior, modest reframing, contextual focus, and ethical ambiguity expressed through ordinary care.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene around a care gesture that complicates a family grievance; observe at ordinary household height, keep at least two overlapping routines active, use shared window and practical light, and withhold judgment without formal emptiness.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: siblings sort their late mother's kitchen after the funeral.
- Translation: one washes an unused bowl while another quietly hides a bill in a drawer; an eye-level doorway view keeps the third sibling carrying boxes through the rear room under rainy window light and a kitchen fixture.

## 导演：hou-hsiao-hsien

# Hou Hsiao-hsien

## Identity
- Chinese name: 侯孝贤
- Region: Taiwan
- Representative works: *A City of Sadness*, *Millennium Mambo*, *The Assassin*
- Best suited for: duration, historical residue, layered everyday space.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Let time remain in the frame. A conversation, meal, transition, or household task continues across layers while the central feeling is only partly available.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: accept daylight, room practicals, street spill, or atmospheric exterior light already present in the location.
- Shadow and contrast: keep contrast restrained but spatially varied as thresholds and distance absorb light.
- Highlights and visibility: light the room's duration rather than a protagonist; figures may recede behind curtains, smoke from real activity, or doorways.
- Reject: ornamental haze, portrait keys, theatrical shafts, or uniform low-contrast prettiness.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use muted material color and period-specific surfaces; allow one source or costume color to persist without dominating.
- Skin and midtones: let skin share the room's ambient or practical response, sometimes softened by distance.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: preserve quiet midtones, dense threshold blacks, and gentle highlight roll-off without faded-memory grading.
- Reject: museum sepia, cyan nostalgia, decorative desaturation, or luxurious historical color.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use sustained medium-long or distant normal-to-long observation that keeps multiple planes active.
- Height and position: place the camera in an adjacent room, across a courtyard, or behind a threshold at a stable human height.
- Movement and focus: remain still or move minimally; favor middle-to-deep spatial focus and let action drift in and out of legibility.
- Witness role: a patient resident who cannot control events; reject empty surveillance distance and explanatory coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: distribute attention across ongoing activity rather than isolate a declarative gesture.
- Layers and obstruction: organize rooms, doors, windows, furniture, fabric, and partial bodies as inhabited depth.
- Blocking and geometry: let people enter, leave, sit, serve, and speak across planes without facing the camera.
- Information control: important dialogue or action may be partly obscured while environment and duration remain readable.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a transition or ordinary activity continuing after news arrives.
- Character action: clear bowls, cross a doorway, wait, or attend to work while emotion remains indirect.
- Visual center: let several modest actions compete with the nominal subject.
- Blocking: distribute people across thresholds and depth.
- Environment: keep background life moving at its own pace.

## Capture Texture
Use quiet unforced capture, stable spatial focus, material air, and exposure that belongs to the location. Texture records duration and distance, not prestige stillness.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating a distant shot as empty.
- Freezing actors in a museum tableau.
- Adding artificial mist for atmosphere.
- Making historical space ornamental.
- Using long takes without layered activity.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Tsai Ming-liang, Hou fills duration with layered social life: light and color circulate through inhabited rooms, the camera observes several planes rather than isolating a body in architectural vacancy, and composition hides information through ongoing activity rather than severe stillness and absence.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use sustained observational distance, layered rooms and thresholds, restrained source-based light, muted material color, middle-to-deep focus, partial information, and everyday actions unfolding on several planes while time passes through the environment.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect away from a declarative gesture toward duration shared by layered rooms; hold a stable medium-long witness through thresholds, let several modest actions continue, keep light and color location-bound, and allow the nominal subject to remain partly absorbed or obscured.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a family dinner continues after difficult news.
- Translation: one person clears bowls while another stays seated and a third crosses the rear room; a stable view from the next room keeps all three planes active under weak window light and a dining fixture.
