# 电商产品展示 - 提示词规范参考

## 一、英文提示词书写原则

LibTV视频生成节点使用英文提示词，必须遵循以下规范：

### 基础结构公式
```
[Camera/Shot Type] + [Movement] + [Product Description] + [Action/Demo] + [Lighting] + [Background/Setting] + [Quality Parameters]
```

### 各模块详解

**1. Camera/Shot Type（景别，必须放最前面）**
- `Extreme close-up shot` / `ECU` - 大特写
- `Close-up shot` / `CU` - 特写
- `Medium shot` / `MS` - 中景
- `Wide shot` / `WS` - 全景
- `Top-down shot` - 俯拍
- `Low angle shot` - 仰拍
- `3/4 angle shot` - 3/4侧角
- `Flat lay shot` - 平铺俯拍

**2. Movement（运镜，紧跟景别）**
- `slow push in` - 缓推
- `slow pull out` - 缓拉
- `360-degree orbit rotation` - 360环绕
- `static, no camera movement` - 固定机位
- `tracking shot following the hand` - 跟随手部
- `slow pan left/right` - 缓慢摇镜
- `snap zoom in` - 急推（用于钩子开场）
- `dolly zoom` - 滑动变焦（慎用）

**3. Product Description（产品外观锚定，最重要的模块）**

必须精确描述，让AI严格还原参考图：
- 颜色（精确色名：`burgundy red`而非`red`，`matte black`而非`black`）
- 材质（`matte plastic`, `brushed aluminum`, `clear glass bottle`, `soft silicone`）
- 形状（`cylindrical`, `rectangular with rounded corners`, `sleek curved body`）
- 关键视觉特征（`white cap with gold rim`, `LED digital display`, `transparent window showing red liquid inside`）
- 品牌Logo位置（如有参考图必须标注：`brand logo visible on the front center`）

⚠️ 注意：产品外观描述必须与用户提供的参考图一致，禁止擅自添加或改变颜色/造型/装饰。

**4. Action/Demo（动作/演示内容）**
- 使用具体动词描述正在发生的事：`water droplets rolling off the surface`, `a hand pressing the button`, `cream being dispensed from the pump`, `steam rising from the cup`
- 避免模糊描述：不说"showing the waterproof feature"，要说"water splashing onto the device and beading up, the device remains fully functional"
- 手部出镜时：`a feminine hand with light skin and neatly trimmed nails holding...`（根据目标人群调整手型）

**5. Lighting（光线）**
- `soft studio lighting from 45 degrees` - 45度柔光
- `dramatic side lighting with strong rim light` - 戏剧侧光+强轮廓光
- `warm golden hour natural light from window` - 暖调自然光
- `bright even lighting, minimal shadows` - 明亮均匀光
- `backlit with light rays through the product` - 逆光穿透
- `cool blue-toned tech lighting` - 冷蓝科技光

**6. Background/Setting（背景）**
- 简洁为主：`pure white background`, `dark gradient background`, `light gray seamless background`
- 场景感：`on a clean marble countertop`, `in a modern minimalist kitchen`, `on a wooden table with soft bokeh of a cozy living room`
- 道具（仅辅助，不能抢产品风头）：`with a small green plant in soft blur background`, `surrounded by fresh fruit slices out of focus`

**7. Quality Parameters（画质参数，每镜必加）**
```
cinematic product photography, 4K resolution, 25fps, photorealistic, sharp focus on product, professional commercial lighting, high-end advertisement aesthetic
```

## 二、负面词（Negative Prompt）

每镜必须追加以下负面词：
```
no text watermark, no logo other than product brand, no distorted product shape, no extra fingers, no extra limbs, no human face close-up, no cartoon style, no anime, no illustration, low quality, blurry, oversaturated, underexposed, overexposed, noise, artifacts, deformed, disfigured
```

品类追加：
- 美妆护肤：`no fake skin texture, no plastic-looking skin, no uncanny valley`
- 食品饮料：`no plastic-looking food, no artificial coloring bleed, no mold`
- 3C数码：`no floating parts, no impossible geometry, no extra buttons`
- 服饰鞋包：`no distorted fabric pattern, no extra buttons or zippers`

## 四、提示词长度控制
- 单镜提示词控制在80-150个英文单词
- 过短（<50词）容易缺乏细节导致渲染不稳定
- 过长（>200词）容易导致AI抓不住重点
- 产品外观描述占30-40%，动作演示占30%，光线背景占20-30%
