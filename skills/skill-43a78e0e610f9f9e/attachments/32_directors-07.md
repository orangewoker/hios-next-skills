## 导演：ingmar-bergman

# Ingmar Bergman

## Identity
- Chinese name: 英格玛·伯格曼
- Region: Sweden
- Representative works: *Persona*, *Cries and Whispers*, *Fanny and Alexander*
- Best suited for: intimacy, silence, faith, identity, family confrontation.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the pause in which speech fails and a face, hand, distance, or shared room reveals an existential or relational rupture.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, overcast sky, candles, lamps, stage or domestic practicals with direct relation to faces and walls.
- Shadow and contrast: permit soft northern tonal range, severe close facial contrast, or deep room shadow according to confrontation.
- Highlights and visibility: eyes, skin, white fabric, walls, and hands receive precise attention while irrelevant space may disappear.
- Reject: generic high-contrast black and white, glamour key light, mystical beams, or theatrical darkness without relational pressure.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use skin, white, black, deep red, wood, muted blue, and domestic materials with psychological restraint.
- Skin and midtones: preserve pores, age, pallor, flushing, and uneven emotion rather than cosmetic neutrality.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: protect facial whites, allow dense relational blacks, and hold nuanced skin midtones; monochrome is optional.
- Reject: automatic desaturation, red-room imitation, sepia solemnity, or skin smoothing.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: move from composed room-wide relation to searching normal or mildly long close observation when silence becomes the event.
- Height and position: stand or sit directly within the interpersonal axis, at table, bed, doorway, stage, or shoreline.
- Movement and focus: hold, approach slowly, or transfer focus between faces and hands; use shallow focus only to intensify relation.
- Witness role: an intimate examiner, not a voyeur or sentimental confidant; reject ornamental camera movement.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: privilege a face-to-face gap, averted gaze, hand, or absent person.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, beds, curtains, mirrors, tables, and dark room depth sparingly.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange frontal confrontation, profile separation, or faces sharing one plane with exact emotional distance.
- Information control: reveal physical emotion closely while withholding certainty about belief, truth, or identity.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the silence after a question or before an admission.
- Character action: look away, touch, withdraw, listen, close eyes, or fail to speak.
- Visual center: make the distance between faces or hand and face decisive.
- Blocking: simplify surrounding action and sharpen relational axis.
- Environment: let domestic or ritual space concentrate the encounter.

## Capture Texture
Use truthful skin, nuanced window or practical exposure, restrained optical sharpness, fabric, wood, and silence-like dark areas. Avoid prestige monochrome and beauty close-ups.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every frame black and white.
- Using extreme close-ups without relational buildup.
- Treating solemnity as depth.
- Copying red rooms or chess imagery.
- Smoothing skin or beautifying anguish.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Ingmar-adjacent theatrical stylists such as Yorgos Lanthimos, Bergman seeks intimate existential exposure: source light serves skin and gaze, color remains psychologically spare, camera approaches the interpersonal axis, and composition reduces to relational distance rather than cold wide distortion, deadpan palette, detached camera, and institutional blocking as absurd control.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use source light that reveals truthful skin, psychologically spare color, an intimate normal-or-mildly-long camera on the interpersonal axis, exact face/hand distance, simplified blocking, and silence after speech fails.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the pause after a question cannot be answered; reduce the frame to the relational axis, approach only as physical emotion becomes the event, preserve pores and nuanced source exposure, and let surrounding room depth fall away without beauty lighting.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: sisters wait beside their father's hospital bed after being asked who will take him home.
- Translation: one folds the blanket while the other keeps her hand above his without touching; a seated bedside view holds both profiles and the untaken hand under grey window light and one dim lamp.

## 导演：jia-zhangke

# Jia Zhangke

## Identity
- Chinese name: 贾樟柯
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Platform*, *Still Life*, *A Touch of Sin*
- Best suited for: social transition, provincial life, labor, changing landscapes.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an ordinary action dwarfed by economic or historical change. Let demolition, construction, transit, commerce, and public routine continue without explaining themselves.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: accept flat daylight, industrial spill, cheap fluorescents, street lamps, and harsh available interiors.
- Shadow and contrast: use unembellished contrast, often broad and low until a practical source creates a blunt local break.
- Highlights and visibility: white sky, bare bulbs, wet concrete, or signage may clip slightly while people remain plainly exposed.
- Reject: elegant noir lighting, sentimental golden hour, polished window keys, or sculpted faces.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep concrete, dust, faded paint, cheap plastics, work clothes, and occasional commercial color materially honest.
- Skin and midtones: preserve ordinary skin under unattractive ambient or fluorescent contamination.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate flat skies, dead midtones, dense interiors, and small exposure mismatches between public spaces.
- Reject: tasteful desaturation, retro nostalgia, cyan industrial grading, or beautified poverty.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor patient medium-wide or lateral long observation with enough context for labor and transition.
- Height and position: stand at street, platform, room, or riverbank eye level where a passerby could wait.
- Movement and focus: hold, pan, or track simply with real movement; retain contextual depth and incidental background activity.
- Witness role: a resident observer, not a detached formalist; reject dramatic push-ins, polished handheld urgency, and heroic aerial scale.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a modest human task compete with construction, demolition, traffic, or public routine.
- Layers and obstruction: use roads, unfinished buildings, shopfronts, vehicles, river traffic, and crowds as historical context.
- Blocking and geometry: people enter and leave without choreography, often small against transitional space.
- Information control: show material change clearly while leaving motives and systems unresolved.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose routine continuing during a social or economic shift.
- Character action: wait, carry, sell, repair, call, or search without performance.
- Visual center: share attention between labor and changing landscape.
- Blocking: allow incidental public movement around the subject.
- Environment: make construction, closure, transit, or commerce visibly ongoing.

## Capture Texture
Use plain available exposure, rough ambient sound-like visual density, ordinary digital or film response, and unbeautified surfaces. Never turn hardship into designed texture.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every frame bleak and empty.
- Beautifying demolition with golden light.
- Using handheld grit as realism.
- Treating provincial characters as symbols.
- Muting all color into tasteful grey.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Edward Yang, Jia privileges historical transition over relational architecture: flatter or rougher available light, more incidental material color, patient street-level context, and bodies absorbed by labor and changing landscapes rather than lucid practical contrast, composed medium distance, and social links mapped through glass and rooms.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use patient street-level observation, blunt available light, ordinary material color, contextual depth, incidental public blocking, and a modest action continuing while demolition, transit, commerce, or institutional change reshapes the space.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as routine persisting inside visible transition; hold a patient eye-level camera with broad context, accept unattractive source light and exposure mismatch, keep incidental public movement, and let the changing landscape rival the person.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a clerk closes a rural bus station ticket window for the last time.
- Translation: she tapes a handwritten notice while passengers continue crossing the forecourt; a static medium-wide view holds faded signs, idling buses, and demolition fencing under flat afternoon light.

## 导演：jiang-wen

# Jiang Wen

## Identity
- Chinese name: 姜文
- Region: China
- Representative works: *In the Heat of the Sun*, *Let the Bullets Fly*, *Devils on the Doorstep*
- Best suited for: volatile power games, masculine bravado, historical absurdity, unreliable memory.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant confidence becomes performance and performance exposes danger. Let competing claims of authority collide through bodies, objects, and sharp tonal reversals.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use forceful sun, hard window light, fire, bare bulbs, and practical interiors with clear directional authority.
- Shadow and contrast: favor bold hard contrast, abrupt tonal shifts, and hot or dark zones that make power unstable.
- Highlights and visibility: sweat, dust, metal, food, and faces can flare under heat while corners hold suspicion.
- Reject: soft tasteful prestige light, even period illumination, heroic rim light, or atmospheric murk.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use sun-baked earth, military or civic materials, hot skin, flags, painted walls, and food as forceful but grounded color.
- Skin and midtones: allow heat, redness, dust, and source contamination to remain physical.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hot highlights, firm blacks, and punchy midtones without commercial gloss.
- Reject: heritage sepia, uniform patriotic red, teal-orange action grading, or nostalgic softness.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use assertive wide or normal proximity for confrontation, with longer lenses for suspicious counter-observation.
- Height and position: occupy the table edge, courtyard, vehicle, or crowd at a bold but physically plausible height.
- Movement and focus: pan, surge, or hold abruptly as authority shifts; maintain enough depth for competing reactions.
- Witness role: an energized participant who distrusts the performance, not a neutral historian; reject weightless spectacle moves.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: make a boast, object, or command compete with contradictory reactions.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, weapons, food, doors, crowds, smoke from real activity, and architecture as pressure.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange power triangles, sudden reversals, and bodies claiming or losing territory.
- Information control: reveal performance clearly while leaving the true balance of power unstable.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant a confident statement becomes dangerous or absurd.
- Character action: raise, slam, toast, point, sit, or refuse.
- Visual center: split attention between the performance and a dissenting reaction.
- Blocking: create a triangle of authority rather than a frontal hero.
- Environment: let heat, crowd, table, vehicle, or courtyard amplify conflict.

## Capture Texture
Use hard sun, sweat, dust, rough practical interiors, and kinetic but readable bodies. Avoid retro polish, patriotic spectacle, and comic-book exaggeration.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating swagger as uncomplicated heroism.
- Making every scene loud or comic.
- Applying sepia to history.
- Using low angles without a power reversal.
- Replacing physical heat with saturated grading.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Zhang Yimou, Jiang Wen destabilizes order from inside performance: harder volatile light, hotter mixed material color, assertive participant camera, and triangular improvised blocking rather than ceremonial source hierarchy, concentrated color masses, measured axes, and repeated graphic choreography.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use volatile hard source light, hot material color, assertive wide-or-normal proximity, competing reactions, power triangles, and a boast or command that tips from confidence into absurd danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a performance of authority begins to fail; put a bold physical camera close to the contested space, keep at least two contradictory reactions visible, use hard heat-driven light and punchy material color, and let blocking reveal the reversal.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a village committee announces a compulsory relocation.
- Translation: the official raises a stamped notice while a farmer calmly moves his chair into the aisle; a wide table-edge witness keeps laughter, alarm, and the blocked exit active under hard noon window light.
