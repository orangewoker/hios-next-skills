## 导演：paul-thomas-anderson

# Paul Thomas Anderson

## Identity
- Chinese name: 保罗·托马斯·安德森
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *There Will Be Blood*, *The Master*, *Phantom Thread*
- Best suited for: power duels, obsessive work, family substitutes, volatile intimacy.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a social or work ritual in which two people negotiate dependence and control. Performance accumulates through gesture, language, and spatial encroachment before a rupture.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, windows, oil lamps, work lamps, dining practicals, stage or institutional sources appropriate to era.
- Shadow and contrast: favor rich naturalistic contrast with faces moving between broad source fields and intimate practical pools.
- Highlights and visibility: skin, fabric, oil, wood, glass, food, and machinery carry tactile highlights without glamour.
- Reject: prestige amber wash, universal backlight, polished period glow, or expressionistic shadow without a power exchange.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive restrained rich color from period materials, skin, work clothes, interiors, landscape, and food.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin textured and responsive to source warmth, fatigue, sweat, or illness.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: preserve deep material blacks, creamy or daylight whites, and substantial midtones.
- Reject: sepia prestige grading, teal-orange period color, pastel romance, or luxurious fabric color without labor.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use medium-wide ensemble space, intimate normal-lens proximity, or measured longer observation for power.
- Height and position: occupy table, workshop, doorway, stage, field, or corridor at participant height.
- Movement and focus: track confidently with entrances or labor, circle or push only as control shifts, and retain contextual focus.
- Witness role: a fascinated participant in the duel, not a detached institution; reject empty long-take display.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: hold two competing wills in one social or work relation.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, tools, doorways, mirrors, corridors, workers, and landscape to support dependency.
- Blocking and geometry: let one person invade, withdraw, mirror, or dominate the other's working territory.
- Information control: show the power exchange while leaving affection and motive contradictory.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a ritual just before control reverses.
- Character action: serve, inspect, rehearse, interrupt, repair, eat, or refuse.
- Visual center: bind two wills through one task or object.
- Blocking: use encroachment and changing distance.
- Environment: make workshop, table, field, or institution carry labor and status.

## Capture Texture
Use substantial skin, cloth, wood, oil, food, machinery, and source-rich exposure. Avoid prestige-film polish and decorative period texture.

## Common Misreadings
- Using long takes without a power duel.
- Making every scene loudly confrontational.
- Applying warm period grading.
- Treating eccentricity as character depth.
- Styling labor and fabric as luxury advertising.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Martin Scorsese, Paul Thomas Anderson sustains bilateral power negotiation: richer stable source light, period/material color, measured tracking or medium-wide staging, and territory shared by two obsessive wills rather than volatile practical shifts, street color, implicated social camera, and one performer exposed by a wider judging network.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use rich source-naturalistic contrast, substantial material color, confident participant camera, two competing wills bound by a task, changing bodily distance, and a work or social ritual approaching reversal.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat before control reverses; keep both wills and the shared task in one spatial relation, let the camera move only with encroachment or withdrawal, and use source-rich skin and material texture without prestige warmth.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a tailor fits a mayor's coat after learning the contract will be canceled.
- Translation: the tailor tightens one sleeve while the mayor continues dictating terms; a measured workshop track keeps scissors, assistants, mirror, and shrinking distance active under north-window light and one work lamp.

## 导演：robert-eggers

# Robert Eggers

## Identity
- Chinese name: 罗伯特·艾格斯
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *The Witch*, *The Lighthouse*, *The Northman*
- Best suited for: historical belief, elemental dread, isolation, ritual and labor.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a task or ritual whose period belief system makes danger materially real to the characters. Weather, labor, language, and architecture trap bodies before the uncanny is confirmed.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast sky, fire, candle, hearth, oil lamp, window, moon or snow reflection only when historically and physically credible.
- Shadow and contrast: accept severe low-light falloff, deep corners, hard fire transitions, and pale exterior fields.
- Highlights and visibility: flame, wet skin, wool, wood, salt, metal, and weather catch limited harsh highlights.
- Reject: modern fill light, blue moonlight presets, fantasy beams, glossy candlelight, or readable darkness without a source.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: restrict color to period cloth, timber, earth, soot, sea, skin, fire, and weather.
- Skin and midtones: let skin appear cold, smoke-stained, fire-warmed, wet, or underexposed according to labor and source.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: permit dense blacks, chalky skies, candle clipping, and narrow midtone range without digital murk.
- Reject: prestige sepia, teal-orange history, desaturated horror filter, or jewel color unsupported by materials.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use severe wide or normal observation for architecture and labor, with controlled closer views for ritual detail.
- Height and position: stand at hut, field, shore, table, deck, forest edge, or ritual circle where period space remains buildable.
- Movement and focus: lock, pan, or move slowly with labor and ritual; preserve physical depth and avoid weightless supernatural motion.
- Witness role: a stern historical observer inside the belief system, not a modern skeptical tourist; reject flashy horror coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a body or ritual object inside oppressive elemental or architectural order.
- Layers and obstruction: use beams, doors, smoke from real fire, trees, sea, weather, tools, and animals.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange labor and ritual in severe lines, clusters, or circles whose logic belongs to the period.
- Information control: show material hardship and belief clearly while withholding whether the threat is supernatural.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose labor or ritual immediately before belief becomes action.
- Character action: tend, bind, cut, pray, haul, listen, or refuse.
- Visual center: connect body, tool, and elemental pressure.
- Blocking: keep community order or isolation physically severe.
- Environment: make weather, fire, sea, forest, or architecture constrain survival.

## Capture Texture
Use historically credible low light, rough wool, timber, soot, wet skin, weather, and grounded optical severity. Avoid prestige antiquing and horror polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every scene monochrome.
- Adding blue moonlight.
- Using candles beyond plausible brightness.
- Treating dialect, costume, or dirt as the style.
- Revealing the supernatural too clearly.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Lynch, Eggers keeps dread historically and materially constrained: source-poor elemental light, restricted earthbound color, severe stable wide-or-normal camera, and ritual bodies trapped by belief rather than theatrical practical pools, saturated psychological color, creeping subjective camera, and identity rupturing across domestic dream seams.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use historically credible source-poor light, restricted material color, severe wide-or-normal observation, elemental pressure, period labor or ritual, dense falloff, and belief becoming action before the uncanny is confirmed.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the task or ritual just before belief dictates danger; place a stern camera inside a buildable period space, limit illumination to credible sources, let body, tool, and weather form the composition, and keep the supernatural unconfirmed.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: lighthouse keepers discover the supply boat has not arrived.
- Translation: one counts the final oil tins while the other bars the storm door; a severe wide interior uses one lamp, white window surf, black rafters, wet wool, and the staircase as an unresolved route.

## 导演：shunji-iwai

# Shunji Iwai

## Identity
- Chinese name: 岩井俊二
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Love Letter*, *All About Lily Chou-Chou*, *Hana and Alice*
- Best suited for: youth, seasonal memory, fragile friendship, private connection.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a small afterimage of change rather than a declaration. Hesitation, unattended objects, weather, and distance let emotion remain vulnerable and unfinished.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast window light, low winter sun, school fluorescents, or modest room practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: favor soft or slightly unstable contrast with real darker passages behind glass, corridors, or weather.
- Highlights and visibility: permit pale window bloom or snow reflection while keeping faces and ordinary interiors imperfectly exposed.
- Reject: beauty haze, magical rays, uniform high-key softness, or music-video backlight.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive pale seasonal color from sky, snow, school paint, clothing, foliage, and available sources.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin natural, sometimes cool or softly underexposed within the season.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: allow restrained white bloom and quiet blacks while preserving used materials and non-pastel neutrals.
- Reject: pink nostalgia filters, pastel decoration, cyan dream wash, or overexposure as a universal rule.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use intimate normal-lens observation or a gentle remove that keeps weather and empty space present.
- Height and position: watch from a corridor, doorway, window, room edge, or plausible handheld distance.
- Movement and focus: use restrained handheld instability, small reframing, or stillness; softness must follow exposure, air, glass, or distance.
- Witness role: a close friend reluctant to intrude, not a music-video spectator; reject sweeping moves and synthetic bokeh.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: privilege a small gesture or unattended object against seasonal empty space.
- Layers and obstruction: use doors, windows, school furniture, glass, and weather as gentle separation.
- Blocking and geometry: keep young people vulnerable, off-center, and often looking elsewhere rather than performing.
- Information control: show emotional residue while leaving the relationship unstated and faces selectively incomplete.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the final routine after a departure or before recognition.
- Character action: sort, pack, wait, read a name, or look away.
- Visual center: let an object or hesitation share the frame with the person.
- Blocking: preserve quiet distance between people or between person and remembered place.
- Environment: make season and ordinary architecture hold the afterimage.

## Capture Texture
Use pale material response, imperfect available light, gentle focus softness caused by air or glass, and tactile ordinary surfaces. Avoid dreamy filters and polished youth advertising.

## Common Misreadings
- Adding petals or snow regardless of season.
- Using forced dreamy haze.
- Overexposing every face.
- Turning students into fashion models.
- Treating memory as pastel decoration.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Hirokazu Kore-eda, Iwai makes memory more subjective and season-fragile: paler unstable exposure, softer source bloom, more intimate or glass-separated observation, and objects as emotional afterimages rather than warm-neutral household light, flexible eye-level family observation, overlapping behavior, and ethical everyday continuity.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a vulnerable everyday gesture, season-specific available light, pale material color, intimate normal-lens or gently distant observation, quiet separation through glass or weather, and softness caused by exposure and air rather than a dream filter.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene around a small gesture after change; observe without intrusion through a plausible doorway or glass layer, keep seasonal empty space active, let available light bloom imperfectly, and preserve natural skin and used surfaces without pastel nostalgia.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a student packs the final notebook after everyone leaves.
- Translation: she pauses over a name inside the cover; a corridor doorway view keeps her small among used desks under late overcast window light and one unsteady fluorescent, with the page clearer than her expression.
