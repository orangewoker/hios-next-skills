## 导演：king-hu

# King Hu

## Identity
- Chinese name: 胡金铨
- Region: China / Hong Kong / Taiwan
- Representative works: *A Touch of Zen*, *Dragon Inn*, *Come Drink with Me*
- Best suited for: spatially legible action, thresholds, martial suspense, landscape and architecture.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the pause in which fighters read a space before movement erupts. Architecture, terrain, sound-implied offscreen threat, and precise entrances make action intelligible.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, courtyard sky, windows, lanterns, fire, and weather that belong to inn, temple, forest, or road.
- Shadow and contrast: preserve clear zones for spatial reading, with hard threshold shadows and moving patches from foliage or structure.
- Highlights and visibility: blades, fabric, dust, wood, and faces catch brief directional highlights during movement.
- Reject: fantasy energy glow, universal backlight, murky battle scenes, or modern action-rim lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive restrained but distinct color from costume rank, timber, earth, vegetation, plaster, and practical fire.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin integrated with daylight or lantern sources, not cosmetically isolated.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold readable midtones for action, dense doorway blacks, and protected daylight or flame highlights.
- Reject: wuxia candy color, teal-orange spectacle, sepia antiquity, or monochrome ink-paint imitation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide or normal spatial clarity for action geography, with measured closer views for a hand, glance, or concealed weapon.
- Height and position: stand beside a gate, courtyard edge, inn table, roofline, or forest path where routes remain plausible.
- Movement and focus: hold, pan, or cut through motivated directional changes; preserve deep spatial cues and avoid weightless camera acrobatics.
- Witness role: a tactically attentive observer, not a fighter-mounted action camera.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: make a threshold, line of sight, or concealed entrance as important as the combatant.
- Layers and obstruction: use screens, pillars, doors, rooflines, trees, curtains, and furniture to reveal and conceal routes.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph entrances, pauses, feints, and directional bursts across clear axes and vertical levels.
- Information control: let viewers know the space but not always the hidden opponent's timing.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the listening pause before an ambush or the instant a route is recognized.
- Character action: stop, listen, test, conceal, or cross a threshold.
- Visual center: privilege spatial intelligence over impact.
- Blocking: establish multiple possible entries and one decisive line.
- Environment: make architecture or terrain determine tactics.

## Capture Texture
Use physical fabric, dust, timber, foliage, grounded momentum, and readable motion. Avoid wire-fantasy weightlessness and digital action polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Reducing the method to swordplay.
- Making movement weightless.
- Cutting too fast to read geography.
- Styling historical spaces as museum sets.
- Using smoke and backlight as wuxia shorthand.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Zhang Yimou, King Hu prioritizes tactical thresholds over mass spectacle: more even readable action light, restrained material color, grounded wide camera geography, and entrances across layered architecture rather than dominant chromatic masses, ceremonial contrast, elevated axes, and repeated collective choreography.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a listening pause, readable wide-or-normal action geography, source-based threshold light, restrained costume and material color, layered architecture, concealed entrances, and precise directional blocking before physical release.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat before action, when a character reads the space; place a grounded camera where all viable routes can be understood, use doors, pillars, terrain, and shadows to conceal timing, and let one decisive entrance break the still geometry.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a courier suspects an ambush inside a roadside repair shop.
- Translation: she stops with a parcel halfway across the doorway as a hanging chain moves in the rear bay; a wide counter-edge view maps two exits, a loft, and the sunlit yard under practical shop shadow.

## 导演：kiyoshi-kurosawa

# Kiyoshi Kurosawa

## Identity
- Chinese name: 黑泽清
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Cure*, *Pulse*, *Tokyo Sonata*
- Best suited for: invisible threat, estrangement, ordinary spaces becoming unsafe.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the moment a familiar room or routine reveals a gap in causality. Threat is often offscreen, delayed, or spatially misplaced; characters notice too little or too late.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use flat daylight, weak fluorescents, corridor spill, bare rooms, and practical night sources.
- Shadow and contrast: combine ordinary readable light with one spatially wrong dark zone, unlit doorway, or depth break.
- Highlights and visibility: windows and pale walls may feel exposed while a distant recess refuses information.
- Reject: horror underlighting, colored monster glow, theatrical fog, or darkness spread evenly for mood.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep institutional beige, pale green, concrete, faded domestic color, and weak exterior blue restrained.
- Skin and midtones: let skin look drained by ordinary ambient light rather than supernatural grading.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold pale whites, flat midtones, and a few dense unreadable blacks that have spatial purpose.
- Reject: blue horror filters, green digital sickness, high-contrast monochrome, or desaturation without a threatening gap.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide or normal medium distance that preserves room geometry and offscreen possibility.
- Height and position: stand at a doorway, corridor, room edge, street, or office where an exit and a blind zone coexist.
- Movement and focus: lock off or move slowly after the character; keep deep context and let the threat enter without emphasis.
- Witness role: a calm observer unable to warn the character, not a frightened handheld victim; reject jump-scare push-ins.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place ordinary action beside an empty zone that may become causally active.
- Layers and obstruction: use doors, curtains, corridors, windows, partitions, and walls with uncertain continuity.
- Blocking and geometry: keep bodies separated from exits or from one another by unremarkable architecture.
- Information control: show the room clearly but withhold what makes one area dangerous.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant routine continues after an unexplained sign appears.
- Character action: enter, wait, clean, call, or turn toward a sound without immediate reaction.
- Visual center: split attention between mundane task and spatial absence.
- Blocking: leave a plausible route for threat or disappearance.
- Environment: make ordinary architecture create the causal gap.

## Capture Texture
Use plain exposure, weak institutional color, stable depth, and unshowy surfaces. Horror comes from spatial logic, not texture, fog, or digital decay.

## Common Misreadings
- Darkening the whole frame.
- Adding ghosts visibly.
- Using blue-green horror grading.
- Relying on jump-scare framing.
- Making empty space abstract rather than architectural.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Lynch, Kiyoshi Kurosawa keeps the uncanny spatially plain: flatter ordinary source light, drained institutional color, stable wide observation, and one causal void inside readable architecture rather than saturated dream light, unstable tonal shifts, subjective camera dislocation, and symbolic domestic fragments.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use ordinary flat source light, restrained institutional color, stable wide-or-normal room geometry, calm observation, a mundane action, and one unreadable architectural zone that introduces an invisible causal threat.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat after an unexplained sign but before panic; hold a calm camera where the room and exit are readable, keep the task ordinary, and allow one doorway, corridor, or depth zone to remain dark and causally wrong without horror lighting.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an office cleaner hears a printer start after shutdown.
- Translation: she keeps wiping a desk while paper emerges in the empty rear room; a locked wide view shows the fluorescent office, open exit, and one unlit copy-room doorway without moving closer.

## 导演：lee-chang-dong

# Lee Chang-dong

## Identity
- Chinese name: 李沧东
- Region: South Korea
- Representative works: *Secret Sunshine*, *Poetry*, *Burning*
- Best suited for: moral injury, social ambiguity, grief, ordinary life under unseen pressure.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an ordinary action after a moral or emotional rupture. Let performance, social setting, and withheld knowledge coexist until a small gesture exposes contradiction.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, apartment windows, shops, classrooms, churches, fields, and plain practical interiors.
- Shadow and contrast: keep natural moderate contrast, allowing a face or room to become briefly harsh when circumstance changes.
- Highlights and visibility: sun, pale sky, cheap interiors, and ordinary reflective surfaces remain unromantic.
- Reject: grief backlight, elegant noir, spiritual glow, or darkness used to manufacture ambiguity.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve ordinary Korean streets, skin, clothing, vegetation, interiors, and seasonal weather without beautification.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin emotionally exposed but naturally embedded in ambient light.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: use honest midtones, occasional harsh whites, and unforced blacks; tolerate mundane exposure.
- Reject: tasteful melancholy grade, green social realism, golden redemption color, or muted prestige palette.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use normal or mildly long medium observation, with wider context when social setting complicates the subject.
- Height and position: stay at ordinary eye level in rooms, streets, fields, or public gatherings.
- Movement and focus: hold patiently or reframe minimally; focus remains contextual and does not announce meaning.
- Witness role: an ethically attentive observer who cannot resolve judgment, not a sentimental confidant; reject expressive camera consolation.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a vulnerable gesture within an ordinary social context that may contradict it.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, traffic, neighbors, gatherings, fields, and household objects without pictorial emphasis.
- Blocking and geometry: let people interrupt, misunderstand, watch, or continue routine around the subject.
- Information control: reveal behavior clearly while withholding motive, guilt, or definitive interpretation.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the ordinary task after rupture rather than the rupture itself.
- Character action: write, clean, wait, attend, look, or continue work.
- Visual center: privilege contradiction between gesture and context.
- Blocking: keep bystanders or social routine present.
- Environment: let mundane life resist emotional closure.

## Capture Texture
Use natural skin, ordinary daylight, plain interiors, stable focus, and unembellished temporal patience. Avoid poetic polish and visual pity.

## Common Misreadings
- Turning grief into beauty.
- Using ambiguity as empty mystery.
- Isolating the protagonist from social context.
- Adding sentimental close-ups.
- Applying muted prestige color.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Hirokazu Kore-eda, Lee Chang-dong is more morally abrasive and isolating: harder ordinary daylight, less comforting color, patient medium distance, and contradictory social context around a solitary gesture rather than flexible household light, warm-neutral materials, overlapping family blocking, and care distributed across routines.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use plain source light, ordinary ungraded color, patient eye-level medium observation, a vulnerable action inside contradictory social context, contextual focus, and moral ambiguity without visual consolation.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the mundane action after emotional rupture; hold an ethically attentive camera at ordinary distance, keep bystanders or routine active, use honest daylight or practical exposure, and let one gesture remain clear while motive and judgment stay unresolved.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a teacher returns to class after learning a former student has disappeared.
- Translation: she erases yesterday's assignment while students enter and talk behind her; an eye-level rear-room view keeps chalk dust, white window light, and her stopped hand within otherwise ordinary activity.

## 导演：lou-ye

# Lou Ye

## Identity
- Chinese name: 娄烨
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Suzhou River*, *Summer Palace*, *Blind Massage*
- Best suited for: unstable intimacy, urban desire, political pressure, bodies in motion.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant emotion disrupts a person's planned action. The camera stays physically implicated as bodies, crowds, rooms, and weather make intimacy unstable.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: rely on available windows, fluorescents, street lamps, bars, headlights, and overcast daylight.
- Shadow and contrast: accept abrupt changes, blocked faces, flare, and uneven local contrast as the camera struggles through real space.
- Highlights and visibility: practicals may clip or streak; skin appears and disappears rather than receiving a controlled key.
- Reject: arranged neon beauty, stable romance lighting, glossy darkness, or expressive light without camera obstruction.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep mixed urban source color restless and sometimes dirty, with no single elegant palette.
- Skin and midtones: allow imperfect skin exposure, source contamination, and movement-driven shifts.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate crushed passages, clipped practicals, changing white balance, and rough adaptation when physically caused.
- Reject: Wong-style decorative mixed color, teal-orange grit, uniform green cast, or polished film emulation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use close normal-to-wide proximity or crowded medium views that preserve bodily pressure.
- Height and position: remain shoulder-close, behind bodies, in doorways, vehicles, crowds, or cramped rooms.
- Movement and focus: use restless handheld following, abrupt reframing, occlusion, focus lag, and physically earned blur.
- Witness role: an implicated companion who can lose sight of the subject; reject elegant floating motion and detached long-lens romance.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a body, touch, or loss of contact displace the planned subject.
- Layers and obstruction: use shoulders, crowds, glass, doorways, vehicles, and walls as collisions, not decorative frames.
- Blocking and geometry: people cross camera, separate unpredictably, and pull focus through unstable space.
- Information control: allow faces and events to become incomplete because the witness cannot maintain access.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant a private impulse interrupts public behavior.
- Character action: follow, turn, push through, withdraw, touch, or lose someone.
- Visual center: privilege the bodily rupture over environmental beauty.
- Blocking: keep other bodies capable of blocking or redirecting the camera.
- Environment: make crowds, rain, traffic, or cramped rooms change access.

## Capture Texture
Use physically stressed handheld motion, focus lag, source flare, uneven exposure, and bodily proximity. Avoid applying grime or blur as a universal surface effect.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating all handheld footage as expressive.
- Adding random blur and flare.
- Styling urban desire as neon romance.
- Making camera instability spatially impossible.
- Confusing rough exposure with low-quality texture.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Wong Kar-wai, Lou Ye sacrifices pictorial control to bodily access: light changes more abruptly, color is less patterned, the close handheld camera can lose its subject, and composition is broken by collision rather than arranged through reflective compression and selective temporal smear.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use implicated shoulder-close observation, unstable available light, restless mixed color, handheld following, focus lag, bodily obstruction, and a private impulse disrupting public action. Do not polish instability into neon romance.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant emotion breaks the planned action; keep a physically possible camera close enough to be blocked or redirected, allow focus and exposure to lag through available sources, and make the lost bodily connection—not atmospheric city color—the visual center.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: two former lovers see each other across a crowded hospital corridor.
- Translation: one turns before speaking and the camera is pushed behind passing nurses; fluorescent light clips on white coats, focus catches a shoulder instead of the face, and the distance closes then breaks.
