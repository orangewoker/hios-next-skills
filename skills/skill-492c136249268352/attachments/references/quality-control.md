# Quality control

## Mandatory gates — pass before scoring

All gates must pass. Revise the concept if any answer is “no.”

- **Rare opening:** Is the first camera position genuinely scarce, rather than a front, eye-level, ordinary 45-degree, tabletop, or lifestyle-wide view?
- **Giant-scale default:** Unless another scarce view is more truthful, does the opener make the product architectural with at least three explicit scale cues?
- **Recognition bridge:** Does a distinctive component, silhouette, label zone, or color make the product recognizable within about 2–3 seconds?
- **Evidence-bound copy:** Does the first line begin from a visible fact and avoid unsupported claims or imitation of a known Apple line?
- **Original brand identity:** Would the copy still make sense under the user’s own brand, without Apple names, exact slogans, Pro/Pro Max borrowing, or Apple-like typography?
- **Originality distance:** When a reference ad/page is supplied, have at least three recognizable codes changed among grid, palette, type system, card geometry, CTA, tier names, and animation order/timing?

Score 0–2 on each criterion. Target at least 16/20 and no zero in identity, claims, or timing.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product identity | drifts/invented | mostly stable | exact lock repeated and enforceable |
| Claim truth | invented/unsupported | vague caveat | all claims sourced or marked conceptual |
| Visual hierarchy | noisy/no focal point | mixed | one clear claim and dominant motion per shot |
| Rare viewpoint | conventional or gimmicky | scarce but weakly evidenced | scarce, monumental, and legible with 3+ scale cues |
| Material truth | wrong finish | acceptable | light specifically proves real material |
| Timing | does not add up | sums but crowded | exact sum with readable actions/copy |
| Three-motion use | three equal chaotic motions | incomplete hierarchy | dominant/support/micro hierarchy |
| Edit continuity | arbitrary cuts | some matches | explicit anchors across all cuts |
| Copy/sound | detached or excessive | partly synchronized | proof, copy, and sound land together |
| Generation readiness | mood-only | some specifics | start/action/end, camera, light, negatives complete |

## Final checks

- Durations and timecodes sum exactly to total.
- Mandatory opening gates all pass before numeric scoring.
- F01–F04 of a 4x4 grid form a rare-view recognition ladder, not four ordinary hero angles.
- Every shot has one information task.
- Product becomes recognizable early enough for the platform.
- Full hero view appears at least once.
- Reference geometry, labels, colors, and component counts are locked.
- Camera, subject, and light movements have different roles and amplitudes.
- Every cut has a stated match or intentional contrast.
- Copy is readable and placed in reserved negative space.
- Text generation is not trusted without verification.
- Sound events support structure rather than decorate every motion.
- Negative prompts do not conflict with desired content.
- No Apple logo, exact slogan, copied packaging, proprietary typeface imitation, or false endorsement.
- No decorative use of `Pro / Pro Max` or Apple model architecture unless those are the product’s actual authorized names.
- AE text, chips, model labels, selectors, prices, and CTAs remain live post-production layers and have been character-checked.
- A lineup ending uses the user's own information hierarchy; it does not trace a supplied four-equal-card store layout.
- The final board changes at least three recognizable codes from the supplied reference and records which codes changed.
- A parody or spoof includes a truthful non-official disclosure when distribution context could cause confusion.
- A short film uses one coherent cadence family; it does not stack “重新定义＋再一次＋岂止于＋唯一的是” into parody.
- The result still expresses the user’s own brand/product identity.

## Common failure repairs

| Failure | Likely cause | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Looks generically “cinematic” | adjectives without physical direction | specify geometry, light source, path, surface evidence |
| Product mutates | identity lock missing or too late | repeat lock first; use reference; generate separate frames |
| Motion feels busy | all three channels dominant | freeze one channel and reduce another to micro-motion |
| Black product disappears | no luminance separation | use edge strips, negative/positive fill layers, mid-gray option |
| White product looks gray | underexposed high-key field | preserve white balance; use negative fill for contour |
| Macro is unreadable | no recognition ladder | cut from abstract detail to recognizable feature sooner |
| Copy feels pasted on | no safe area or motion link | reserve space in composition and synchronize entrance to endpoint |
| 4x4 grid has wrong panel count | contact-sheet generation limitation | generate 16 frames separately and composite |
| Chinese text is garbled | model text limitation | render no text; add verified typography in post |
| “Apple style” overwhelms brand | copied cues rather than principles | return to product promise, own colors, own type, original ending |
| Opening feels ordinary | camera begins at eye level or shows full product too early | move camera to the base/interior/surface; crop beyond frame; add 3 scale cues |
| Giant view feels fake | distortion or miniature people do all the work | preserve geometry; use parallax, light travel, depth, and truthful references |
| “岂止于” line feels empty | second clause has no proof | replace it with a user-provided benefit or a neutral sensory statement |
| Copy feels like a counterfeit campaign | exact phrase, tier name, punctuation, and typography all point to Apple | keep the cadence mechanism; rewrite nouns, verbs, tier names, and lockup for the user’s brand |
| Every card sounds the same | repeated “再一次/重新定义” stems | select one cadence family and vary proof lines with concrete nouns and verbs |
| Model reveal feels borrowed | unrelated product is named Pro/Pro Max | use the real product hierarchy or create original descriptive tiers |
| Ending page resembles a traced store page | same equal-card grid, CTA color/wording, type scale, and selector placement | switch to 1+3 asymmetry; change palette, type system, card geometry, CTA, and animation order |
| AE copy feels mechanical | every character animates with the same preset | animate phrase units; use tag, verdict, proof, and naming recipes with evidence-linked timing |
| Feature chips feel decorative | labels do not follow the visible proof path | reorder chips by camera/light revelation and remove unsupported labels |
