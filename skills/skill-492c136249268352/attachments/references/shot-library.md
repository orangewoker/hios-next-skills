## 1. Opening templates

Each template is a reusable structure, not a finished prompt.

Opening is mandatory, not optional. Choose O03 or O11 by default for giant scale. Choose O04, O05, O07, or O12 only when macro/interior/light structure is more truthful to the product. O01, O02, O06, O08, O09, and O10 require an added scarce camera position; their ordinary versions do not pass the opening gate.

Never begin with a complete front, eye-level, tabletop, conventional 45-degree, or lifestyle-wide hero. Make the first 2–3 seconds follow `impossible clue → monumental or microscopic scale evidence → recognizable product feature`.

| ID | Name | 0–40% reveal | 40–75% articulation | 75–100% resolve | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O01 | Black-edge dawn | one rim catches from darkness | edge sweep reveals controls | three-quarter hero | dark metal/plastic |
| O02 | White contour | edge-on object nearly disappears | soft rotation reveals thickness | clean frontal lock | pale, thin products |
| O03 | Monument rise | extreme low angle tracks upward | macro cut across key seam | pullback to full scale | tall or slab products |
| O04 | Probe traversal | macro lens travels a groove/port | feature becomes recognizable | match to wide hero | precision details |
| O05 | Axial tunnel | camera enters circular/square opening | internal layers pass by | exits into full product | lenses, rings, openings |
| O06 | Orthographic bloom | top view, components as geometry | components align/expand | complete plan-view composition | kits, modular parts |
| O07 | Light before object | only a moving highlight is visible | highlight draws silhouette | object emerges after light | glossy/glass surfaces |
| O08 | Color infusion | neutral silhouette | product color spreads through light | final true color holds | color-led launch |
| O09 | Suspended assembly | parts float in depth | axial snap/soft magnetic join | assembled hero | earbuds, bottles, gadgets |
| O10 | Shadow clue | abstract shadow crosses field | source object enters frame | shadow and object align | strong silhouettes |
| O11 | Scale deception | detail reads as architecture | camera reveals familiar feature | scale resolves in wide | small products |
| O12 | One-line incision | narrow slit of light opens | camera passes through line | line becomes product edge | slim or folding products |

### Giant-scale opening modules

| ID | Name | Camera and crop | Scale evidence | Recognition endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G01 | Product skyscraper | camera at the base, 18–24 mm controlled low angle, top exits frame | vertical parallax, long rim travel, partial silhouette | label/control/cap enters at 2 s |
| G02 | Surface as landscape | 90–120 mm probe macro skims texture or condensation | shallow horizon, grazing light, micro debris only if truthful | seam, logo zone, material boundary |
| G03 | Canyon passage | camera passes between two real product structures | occlusion, depth layers, sound reflection | exits to three-quarter partial hero |
| G04 | Axial interior | camera looks through a real opening, ring, mesh, or mouth | concentric depth, inner light falloff | pulls out to the opening and silhouette |
| G05 | Impossible altitude | orthographic top view with product extending beyond frame | long shadow or aligned geometry, slow descent | full plan-view component layout |
| G06 | Shadow monument | the shadow arrives before the product | slow scale-changing shadow, edge reveal | object and shadow align into identity |

Use at least three scale cues. Do not invent internal structures, tiny people, vehicles, or buildings merely to signal size.

## 3. Feature and mechanism modules

| ID | Name | Use | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | Three-click proof | show three physical actions in matched framing | keep each action readable for ≥0.5 s |
| F02 | Exploded alignment | reveal component relationships | do not invent internal engineering |
| F03 | Texture pilgrimage | macro slide across finish and seam | lock color and grain direction |
| F04 | Port-to-purpose | enter connector/port, cut to resulting benefit | avoid unsupported technical claims |
| F05 | Magnetic choreography | accessories align and settle | describe path and endpoint precisely |
| F06 | Fold/open arc | one continuous mechanical motion | preserve hinge count and geometry |
| F07 | Droplet/particle test | visualize resistance or filtration | label as conceptual unless proven |
| F08 | Size ladder | nested silhouettes or matched hand-free scale cues | give real dimensions if factual accuracy matters |
| F09 | Variant carousel | colors rotate through same locked angle | prevent geometry drift between colors |
| F10 | Before/after field | split or match frame shows state change | keep lighting identical for fair comparison |

## 4. Ending modules

| ID | Ending | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Hero and promise | full product settles; benefit line; name; short sonic bloom |
| E02 | Family alignment | variants/accessories form a measured grid; single line of copy |
| E03 | Edge-to-logo substitute | product edge becomes an original graphic line; brand name appears in post |
| E04 | Color extinguish | color field narrows to product; everything else fades to neutral |
| E05 | Return to opening | recreate opening composition with full information now visible |
| E06 | Functional final act | final mechanism closes/clicks; copy lands on the sound transient |

Do not generate or imitate the Apple logo. “Logo落版” means the user’s supplied brand mark or a clean text lockup.

## 5. Matching-edit library

- **Shape:** lens circle → earbud ring → copy dot.
- **Axis:** upward camera move → component rises in next shot.
- **Direction:** light sweeps left-to-right → next product enters left-to-right.
- **Scale:** macro circular detail expands to become a full top view.
- **Luminance:** white highlight fills frame → cut to white field.
- **Color:** accent surface fills frame → next scene inherits the hue.
- **Occlusion:** product passes camera and creates a natural wipe.
- **Focus:** rack focus ends on blur whose color/shape matches the next shot.
- **Sound:** click, lock, magnetic snap, or low impact bridges the cut.
- **Motion endpoint:** rotating object stops on the same angle where the next shot begins.
